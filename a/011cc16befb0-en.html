Omnivision Unveils Two Global Shutter Image Sensors for Industrial and Robotics Applications

Two new global shutter image sensors in 5- and 12-megapixel variants designed for industrial automation, vehicles, and robotics.

Omnivision has introduced two global shutter image sensors, available in 5- and 12-megapixel variants, tailored for industrial automation, autonomous vehicles, and robotics. Unlike rolling-shutter sensors, which capture images line by line and introduce distortions during rapid movements, global shutter sensors expose the entire image simultaneously, ensuring clarity even under constant vibrations or motion.

The OG05D, the first 5-megapixel sensor in a 1/2.5-inch format with 2.2-micron pixel size, features HDR (high dynamic range) and back-side illumination (BSI) for improved low-light performance. It also supports near-infrared (NIR) via Nyxel technology, RGB, monochrome, and RFB-IR modes, with a maximum frame rate of 146 frames per second in linear mode. The manufacturer claims a 30% reduction in power consumption compared to its previous generation.

The OG12S10, the second model, offers 12 megapixels in a 1.1-inch optical format. While its maximum frame rate is reduced to 75 frames per second, it retains key features such as BSI for low-light sensitivity, HDR with a claimed 74dB dynamic range, and an ultra-short one-microsecond exposure time. Both sensors are showcased at the VISION trade show in Stuttgart (Hall 10, Booth 10D10) and are expected to be available for sampling in November, with general availability by the end of the year.
