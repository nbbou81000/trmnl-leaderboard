Modular Case for ESP32-CYD with Radio Bays and Sensors

A 3D-printed case turns an ESP32-CYD into a modular handheld device, supporting multiple radio and sensor modules.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

A 3D-printed enclosure for the ESP32-CYD (Cheap Yellow Display) turns it into a versatile handheld device. The sturdy design includes a slot for four 18650 batteries and space for a power bank board to enable charging functionality. SMA connectors are provided for multiple antennas, supporting compatibility with various radio modules.

The case features dedicated bays for multiple modules: GPS, NFC, infrared (IR), radio frequency (315/433 MHz), CC1101, NRF24L01, and a speaker. The recommended material is PETG-CF (carbon fiber-reinforced PETG) with a 0.2 mm layer height and 25–30 % infill, balancing strength and weight.

The design prioritizes easy wiring, expansion, and experimentation. A specific variant, called handheld-hacker, allows the CYD to rotate for improved ergonomics. The design files are available for download on MakerWorld.
