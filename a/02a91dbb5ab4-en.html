A thin, snap-together 3D-printed case designed for a third-party Pico W USB-C board with non-standard dimensions is now available as a free download. The enclosure measures 54×24×6 mm and features cutouts for the board’s two onboard buttons and fully enclosed GPIO pins. The STL file is optimized for quick printing: 0.16 mm layer height, 2 walls, 15 % infill, requiring about 16 minutes and 5 g of PLA.

Note that this case is designed specifically for a third-party Pico W USB-C variant (51.50×21.0 mm) and will not fit official Raspberry Pi Pico or Pico 2 W models. Users are advised to verify compatibility with their own board before printing. The files are available for free download on MakerWorld.
