Tektronix Unveils Sub-$500 Digital Oscilloscopes for Hobbyists and Students

Tektronix launches the 1 Series, a budget-friendly digital oscilloscope line starting at $450, targeting students and hobbyists.

Tektronix, a leader in test and measurement equipment, has introduced the 1 Series of digital phosphor oscilloscopes (DPOs), designed to make professional-grade tools accessible to students, educators, and electronics enthusiasts. Priced from $450 for the entry-level model, these oscilloscopes offer advanced features at an affordable cost.

The 1 Series includes models with 2 to 4 channels and bandwidths ranging from 50 to 200 MHz. The base model, the DPO1052, features two channels, a 50 MHz bandwidth, a sampling rate of 1.25 GS/s (or 625 MS/s when using both channels), a 10-million-point record length, and 12-bit vertical resolution. The top-end model, the DPO1204, offers four channels and 200 MHz bandwidth, with intermediate options at 70 MHz and 100 MHz. Some variants include a built-in arbitrary function generator (AFG), indicated by an « S » suffix in the model name.

All models share a 7-inch touchscreen display and a user interface identical to Tektronix’s higher-end oscilloscopes, ensuring consistency and ease of upgrades. They allow disabling automatic modes in favor of manual operation, a feature particularly useful for educational settings. Analysis tools include protocol decoding (SPI, I2C, UART), fast Fourier transform (FFT), limit testing, and—exclusive to the « S » variants—Bode plot generation.

Prices range from $450 for the DPO1052 (2 channels, 50 MHz) to $1,650 for the DPO1204S (4 channels, 200 MHz with AFG).
