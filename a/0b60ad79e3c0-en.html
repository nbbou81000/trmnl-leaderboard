ToolDance X1: A Compact and Rigid 5-Axis CNC Milling Machine Launches on Kickstarter

The ToolDance X1 CNC milling machine, featuring a cast aluminum frame and a 1.5 kW spindle motor, is now available for pre-order on Kickstarter.

The ToolDance X1 CNC milling machine stands out with its cast aluminum frame, reinforced for optimal rigidity despite its compact size. Weighing 110 kg, it offers a working volume of 400 × 265 × 180 mm, expandable with optional modules. Its motion system uses preloaded ball screws and NEMA 23 stepper motors, paired with 14-bit absolute encoders and an RTOS-based real-time controller. This setup ensures 2 kHz interpolation and 200-block lookahead in G-code for enhanced precision.

The spindle motor delivers 1.5 kW and reaches a maximum speed of 24,000 RPM, a performance level unusual for a desktop machine. An automatic tool changer (ATC) handles up to nine tools, identified via RFID, with cutting diameters up to 18 mm and shank diameters up to 10 mm. Additional options include flood coolant, dust collection, an integrated HD camera, a touchscreen interface, and an MPG hand wheel for jogging.

The ToolDance X1 ships as a 3-axis machine by default, but extension modules allow upgrading to 4 or 5 axes. The included AutoCAM software simplifies toolpath generation tailored to the machine, supporting 3-axis, 3+2-axis, and simultaneous 5-axis operations. It also features simulation tools to validate projects before machining. The machine is compatible with a wide range of materials, from wood to titanium and tool steel.

Pre-orders are open on Kickstarter, with an early bird price of $2,999, compared to a retail price of $5,999. Deliveries are scheduled for Q1 2027.
