A Mini Robot to Monitor Plant Moisture in Real Time

A maker going by the name CrazyScience has built a tiny robot planter that monitors soil moisture in real time and displays the results through facial expressions. The project, shared on Instructables, is designed as a playful companion to prevent plants from drying out, especially when users are away or busy.

The robot features a moisture sensor—an electronic component that measures the water content in the soil—whose data is processed instantly. A simple screen or visual system shows the plant’s hydration status using emojis or emoticons, making the information easy to interpret at a glance. The goal is to turn a often-overlooked chore into an engaging, visual interaction that prompts action before the plant suffers from drought.

The project is fully documented on Instructables, with step-by-step instructions for assembly, wiring, and programming. It targets hobbyists and home automation enthusiasts looking to automate plant care without resorting to complex or expensive solutions.
