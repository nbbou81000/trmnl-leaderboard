Rust Firmware for Elgato Key Light with ESP32-C3 and Matter

An open-source Rust firmware replaces the Elgato Key Light’s controller with an ESP32-C3 for local Matter and WiFi control, no cloud.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Michael (@micthiesen) has released key-right, an open-source Rust firmware for GitHub that replaces the stock controller in an Elgato Key Light with an ESP32-C3-MINI-1. The lamp’s PCA9635 LED driver, stock circuitry, LEDs, and power supply remain unchanged. The goal is local control via Apple Home and the Matter protocol over WiFi, without relying on the cloud.

After flashing, each lamp appears as a Matter Color Temperature Light in Apple Home, allowing control of power, brightness, and color temperature. Apple Home’s 1 to 100 percent brightness range is mapped to the lamp’s nominal 1 to 10 percent range, meaning Home’s 100 percent corresponds to a low-light ceiling. The color temperature covers a range of 143 to 344 mired (approximately 6993 K to 2907 K). Multiple lamps can be paired separately and then grouped in Apple Home.

The ESP32-C3 communicates with the PCA9635 over I²C using GPIO4, 5, and 6 for SDA, SCL, and OE signals, and draws power from the lamp’s 3.37 V rail. Firmware version 0.1.4 adds 400 ms eased transitions for smoother brightness adjustments.
