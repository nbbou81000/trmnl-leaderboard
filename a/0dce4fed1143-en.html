Egg Carrier: 3D-Printed Transport Box for 12 M/L Eggs

A 3D-printed transport box safely carrying up to 12 M/L eggs, perfect for chicken owners, campers, or cooks.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

This Egg Carrier model, designed by Solid and available on Makerworld, offers an eco-friendly reusable solution for transporting up to 12 M or L-sized eggs. Individual compartments keep each egg separated and protected from shocks, making it ideal for backyard chicken owners, campers, or cooks looking to avoid disposable cartons.

The assembly relies on a press-fit system for small parts, requiring no glue. For added stability, it is recommended to glue the supports into the bottom part of the box. The closure uses 6× M3×30 screws (4-egg variant: 2× M3×15 + 2× M3×30). Several variants are available: 6 eggs (7 hours of printing), 10 eggs (9.8 hours), or 4 eggs (5.2 hours).
