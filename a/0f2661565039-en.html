ZOTAC Unveils Fanless Industrial Mini PC Powered by Raspberry Pi CM5

ZOTAC introduces a compact, fanless industrial mini PC powered by the Raspberry Pi Compute Module 5, featuring up to 16GB RAM and dual 4K HDMI outputs.

ZOTAC has unveiled the ZBOX PRO CM5-PICO, a compact, fanless industrial mini PC powered by the Raspberry Pi Compute Module 5 (CM5). The system supports up to 16GB of LPDDR4x RAM and offers optional Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.0 connectivity.

The device measures 114.8 × 76 × 31.3 mm and features dual HDMI 2.1 ports capable of 4K @ 60Hz each, a Gigabit Ethernet port with PTP support, and an M.2 2280 socket for NVMe SSDs or AI accelerators. A wide-range 5V to 20V DC input and a 40-pin GPIO header are included, with all I/O ports positioned on one side for easier cable management. The system also supports MIPI CSI-2/DSI camera interfaces and includes a microSD slot on Lite variants.

Designed for industrial automation, digital signage, and IoT gateway applications, the ZBOX PRO CM5-PICO runs Raspberry Pi OS (Debian 13) but is compatible with Ubuntu, Twister OS, Emteria Android, LibreELEC, and Home Assistant OS. The passive aluminum enclosure ensures efficient cooling within an operating temperature range of -20°C to +55°C.

This is ZOTAC’s first CM5-based industrial computer. It stands out with its dual 4K HDMI outputs and compact form factor, ideal for space-constrained environments. The Japanese distributor Ask Corporation lists the product, but pricing and availability details are pending. The product lifecycle is guaranteed until January 2036.
