Rotating Snap-Latch Cable Organizer for Desk Use in 3D Print

A 3D-printed desk cable organizer with rotating tubes and adhesive mounting, supporting up to 7 cables.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

This 3D-printed desk organizer keeps frequently used cables tidy and accessible on the desktop instead of hidden behind the desk. It comes in three configurations: 3, 5, or 7 tubes, which rotate 180° to open or close via a snap-latch spring mechanism. The organizer attaches using double-sided adhesive tape, with a recommendation to use masking tape as an intermediate layer for delicate surfaces.

The assembly is straightforward: the main body snaps onto a base with a specific alignment (the low foot side must face backward). The tubes are removable and replaceable, and multicolor printing is supported without an automatic material system. The V2 update, released in May 2026, features an improved spring mechanism compatible with all filament types.

Design files are available for download on makerworld.com under the model ID 1400922. This project aligns with the 3D printing community’s tradition of sharing electronics-friendly and desk-organization solutions every Thursday under the #3DThursday hashtag.
