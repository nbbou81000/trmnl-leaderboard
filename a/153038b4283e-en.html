Three 3D-Printable Candy Corn Monsters for Halloween

Three candy corn-inspired monsters, available in two sizes, released under CC BY 4.0 license.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Designer Cryostatic introduces three candy corn-inspired monsters—Cornhallow, Huskbinder, and Cobwraith—collectively named the Triadum Inmanducati. According to the associated fiction, these creatures rise at Halloween midnight from leftover candy corn. Each model is designed for 3D printing.

Two sizes are available: a large one at 15 cm (6 inches) tall and a small one at 6.3 cm (2.5 inches). The files are optimized for printing with a 0.2 mm layer height, two walls, and 10 % infill. The large version takes about 27.8 hours to print, while the tiny version requires around 11.7 hours. For the smaller model, Cornhallow’s teeth have been reinforced for better durability.

The files are released under the CC BY 4.0 license, allowing commercial use provided the original creator is credited. They can be downloaded from MakerWorld, a platform dedicated to 3D models. A new subcategory for candy corn monsters has been created for this release.
