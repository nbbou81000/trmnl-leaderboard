Waveshare Unveils ESP32-S31-Zero: Ultra-Compact 320 MHz Board with WiFi 6 & Bluetooth 5.4

Waveshare introduces the ESP32-S31-Zero, a tiny development board powered by a 320 MHz RISC-V SoC, featuring up to 32MB PSRAM and 32MB SPI flash.

Waveshare has launched the ESP32-S31-Zero, a compact development board following the company’s "Zero" form factor, such as the ESP32-C6-Zero or ESP32-S3-Zero. It features Espressif Systems’ ESP32-S31 dual-core RISC-V SoC running up to 320 MHz. The chip supports WiFi 6 (802.11ax), Bluetooth 5.4 (including LE Audio and Bluetooth Mesh), and an 802.15.4 radio for Zigbee, Thread, and Matter protocols.

The board includes 18 through-hole and castellated pins, a USB-C port for power and programming, and a 40-pin high-density connector for expanded I/O access. It offers up to 32MB of PSRAM and 16MB or 32MB of SPI flash, depending on the variant. The module also includes 512KB of regular SRAM and 32KB of low-power SRAM.

The 40-pin connector enables extensions like RGB LCDs, touchscreens, SD cards, USB 2.0 ports, or additional GPIOs. While the ESP32-S31 includes an EMAC RGMII interface for Gigabit Ethernet, these pins are multiplexed with GPIO lines, allowing potential expansion boards to add high-speed wired connectivity. Initial Linux support for the SoC suggests future software maturity for advanced applications.

Waveshare provides technical documentation, PDF schematics, and ESP-IDF code examples covering GPIO control, I2C, WiFi, and Bluetooth LE. As of now, only the ESP32-S31-Zero-N32R16 model (32MB PSRAM, 16MB flash, headers unsoldered) is available, priced at $10.99 on Waveshare’s store, $12.23 on AliExpress, and $18.99 on Amazon.
