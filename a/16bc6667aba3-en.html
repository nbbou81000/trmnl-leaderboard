The Python for Microcontrollers Newsletter is a free weekly publication focused on Python developments for embedded systems, including microcontrollers and single-board computers like the Raspberry Pi. Each issue summarizes the latest software updates, events, projects, and hardware releases worldwide, all in one ad-free and spam-free email.

Sent every Monday around 11 am (US Eastern Time), the newsletter gathers Python-related news you might have missed, covering CircuitPython, MicroPython, and general Python developments, along with recent hardware and software announcements.

Subscriptions are commitment-free and can be canceled anytime. The newsletter also welcomes suggestions and encourages sharing with friends, colleagues, or students interested in Python on hardware.
