ESP32 Rotary Timer with Round Touchscreen Display

A programmable circular timer with touchscreen, ESP32-S3, rotary encoder, and buzzer.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

A new open-source project turns a 2.1-inch rotary touchscreen into a programmable timer. The device pairs an ESP32-S3 with a 480×480 circular touch display and an integrated rotary encoder. Its design echoes smart thermostats like Nest, but the freely available code allows for customization and experimentation.

An PCF8574 I2C I/O expander drives a buzzer for audio feedback when the countdown ends. The rotary encoder lets users set the time by simply turning the ring, while the display updates dynamically, shifting colors as the timer approaches zero for better visibility from a distance.

Once expensive, these circular displays have become more affordable due to the rise of smartwatches. This project joins a growing trend of repurposing such screens for home automation or creative dashboards.
