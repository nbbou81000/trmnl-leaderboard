New STM32G5: 280 MHz Cortex-M33 MCU Targets Power Electronics and Motor Control

STMicroelectronics unveils the STM32G5 family of 280 MHz Cortex-M33 MCUs, doubling STM32G4 performance with math accelerators and dedicated interfaces.

STMicroelectronics has launched the STM32G5 series, a family of Arm Cortex-M33 microcontrollers running at up to 280 MHz, targeting high-performance applications in power electronics, photovoltaic inverters, EV chargers, robotics, and motor control. The new family delivers double the performance of the earlier STM32G4 series, introduced in 2021, with an optimized architecture for intensive computations.

The STM32G5 MCUs feature up to 512 KB of flash memory and 112 KB of ECC-protected SRAM, alongside a high-resolution timer (HRTIM) with 111 ps precision and up to 12 PWM outputs on select variants. The 12-bit ADCs achieve a sampling rate of 5 MSPS, while hardware accelerators like the CORDIC (for trigonometric calculations) and FMAC (for digital filtering) enhance real-time processing. Security is bolstered with Arm TrustZone, secure boot, and post-quantum cryptography support.

Peripherals include up to 103 GPIO pins, I2C/I3C, SPI, USART interfaces, and up to 3 CAN FD controllers. High-end variants offer USB 2.0 Full-Speed, USB Type-C with Power Delivery 3.1, and position encoder interfaces such as EnDat 3.0 or Hiperface DSL. Three development boards are available: the NUCLEO-G564RE (Nucleo-64 with Arduino compatibility), the B-G564E-MC1S (motor control-focused), and the B-G564E-DPOW1 (power electronics-oriented, coming soon).

The MCUs come in packages ranging from 32 to 128 pins, support operating temperatures from -40°C to +125°C, and are certified under SESIP Level 3 and PSA Certified Level 3 (in progress). ST provides the STM32CubeG5 toolchain (HAL, drivers, middleware) and STM32CubeMX for configuration and code generation.
