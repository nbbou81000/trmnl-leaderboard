New Lumissil Cortex-M0+ MCUs with Video Bus and Capacitive Touch Controller

Lumissil introduces two Cortex-M0+ microcontrollers featuring a video bus, LED controller, and capacitive touch controller.

Lumissil Microsystems has launched the IS31CS9202 and IS32CS9202, two 50 MHz Cortex-M0+ microcontrollers targeting industrial, consumer, and automotive applications. The chips integrate a serial video bus (SVB), a LumiBus LED controller, and a capacitive touch-key controller. They feature 16KB of RAM with error correction, 256KB of flash memory with protected information blocks, and an optional SPI flash controller with error correction.

Peripherals include a CAN FD controller up to 10 Mbps, SPI, I2C, and UART interfaces, a quadrature encoder, 16-bit PWM controllers, analog-to-digital converters (14-bit sigma-delta and 12-bit SAR), an 8-bit DAC, and a touch controller offering 24-bit resolution and supporting up to 25 keys (32-pin) or 39 keys (48-pin). The LumiBus controller manages up to 256 addressable RGB LEDs over a single communication channel.

The IS32CS9202 is designed for automotive use, with an extended temperature range of -40°C to 125°C and AEC-Q100 and ASIL-B certifications. Both versions are available in QFN-32, QFN-48, and LQFP-48 packages. Lumissil provides C code examples to utilize peripherals and an IS31CS9202 EBGUI evaluation board with touch interfaces, GPIO headers, and a UART port. Development is supported via Arm Keil MDK or Lumissil’s proprietary IMSC IDE on Windows 10/11.

Documentation and development tools are accessible after registration on the manufacturer’s website, with no manual confirmation required. Pricing is not yet available, but samples can be requested. These microcontrollers target HMI applications, touch-control systems, RGB lighting, and automotive solutions such as center-stack controls or HVAC systems.
