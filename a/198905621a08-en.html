STMicroelectronics Shifts STM32 Focus to Arm Cortex-M MCUs

The manufacturer consolidates its STM32 lineup around Cortex-M microcontrollers to streamline industrial and automotive developments.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

STMicroelectronics has revealed a strategic shift for its STM32 microcontroller lineup, focusing on Arm Cortex-M MCUs to reduce fragmentation across industrial, robotics, automotive, and safety-critical sectors. The unified platform aims to provide continuity between projects, eliminating the need for developers to rebuild designs from scratch, supported by the STM32Cube ecosystem.

The company highlights its collaboration with Arm to shape future Cortex-M core releases, emphasizing their role in edge intelligence. Two new MCU families are introduced: the STM32A series for safety-critical and automotive applications, and the STM32G5 for real-time power control.

The STM32A family includes three models: the STM32A4 for entry-level local control nodes and vehicle safety systems, the STM32A5 for industrial automation and robotics, and the STM32A9 for multi-domain safety-critical use cases. These MCUs are currently sampling, with broader availability expected in 2027. The STM32G5, featuring a 280MHz Arm Cortex-M33 core, is available now at $1.034 per unit in 10,000-unit quantities.
