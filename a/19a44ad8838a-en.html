Modular 3D-Printed Desk Organizer with Custom Accessories

A 3D-printed modular desk organizer with multiple accessories to store phones, headphones, pens and more.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

This project features a fully modular desk organizer designed to accommodate various accessories using a universal clamp (0–40 mm clamping range, 33 mm depth, 4 mm-pitch threads, anti-slip grooves). The core components include a headphone hanger (50 mm shaft), phone holder, cup holder (80 mm inner diameter), 5 mm Skadis section, cable organizer (up to 3 cables), pen holder (50×50×80 mm), two-stage hook, and tray (62×67 mm).

Additional parts expand the system’s versatility: Skadis adapter, storage box, trash bin, solder wire holder, GoPro mount, PS5 controller holder, watch stand, MagSafe holder (53° angle), Apple Watch charging stand, and longer clamps. The design relies on mortise-and-tenon joints reinforced with snap-fit connections.

Three versions are available: the default version (~12.5 hours of printing / 9 plates), the New Parts version (~16.8 hours / 12 plates), and a No-Logo version (~7.9 hours / 9 plates). Files can be downloaded from MakerWorld.
