Geekworm Unveils UPS Scap 5V5A Supercapacitor UPS HAT for Raspberry Pi 5

A supercapacitor-based UPS HAT for Raspberry Pi 5 provides a few seconds to a few minutes of backup power for safe shutdowns.

Geekworm, in collaboration with Mcuzone, has released the UPS Scap 5V5A, a HAT (Hardware Attached on Top) module designed for the Raspberry Pi 5. Its purpose is to provide a few seconds to a few minutes of backup power to safely save data and shut down during power outages, rather than offering hours of continuous operation. Three versions are available, differing in supercapacitor capacity: 100F, 60F, and 22F.

The module includes a power management chip (PD chip) that ensures a stable 5V/5A output, preventing low-voltage warnings common with some power supplies. It connects to the Raspberry Pi 5 via pogo pins to detect power loss and supports I2C for real-time voltage and current monitoring. Dual independent power inputs are provided: a wide-voltage DC barrel jack or a standard USB-C Power Delivery charger.
