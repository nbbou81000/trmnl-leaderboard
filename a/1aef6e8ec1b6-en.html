Turning a Crushed Samsung Galaxy S23 into a Gaming PC

A crushed Samsung Galaxy S23 is repurposed into a functional gaming PC using its Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 SoC and a DIY hack.

A DIY project repurposes a visibly crushed Samsung Galaxy S23 into a working gaming PC. Purchased for just 1,700 Ukrainian hryvnias (~$40), the SIM-locked device was selected for its Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 SoC—a chip also found in high-end gaming handhelds, though typically paired with better cooling.

The transformation involved careful disassembly, hammer strikes to straighten the frame, and a new battery, as the motherboard requires one to function. The enclosure was reworked with a 3D-printed case glued to a 512 GB SSD cage, while two USB hubs (one with HDMI output) provided connectivity. A heatsink was hot-glued to the SoC’s vapor chamber and paired with a fan, reducing temperatures by 7 °C and minimizing thermal throttling. This setup enabled smoother desktop performance via Samsung DeX, the manufacturer’s desktop mode.

Performance-wise, the system handles games like God of War (PS2 emulation) and PC titles through Dolphin and GameHub, which supports Steam, GOG, and other libraries. While alternative OS installations are possible, DeX remains a practical choice for leveraging the phone’s hardware without major software changes.
