The Python for Microcontrollers Newsletter is a weekly publication covering the latest news about Python on embedded hardware (microcontrollers) and single board computers (SBCs like Raspberry Pi).

Each issue, delivered ad-free and spam-free, gathers key updates in one place: software releases, events, projects, and hardware announcements from the past week. Topics include CircuitPython and MicroPython, lightweight Python implementations designed for microcontrollers’ limited resources.

The newsletter is designed for convenience: a weekly summary sent every Monday around 11 am (US Eastern Time), with no commitment. Subscribers can unsubscribe anytime. The publication also encourages sharing with peers, colleagues, students, and others in the community.
