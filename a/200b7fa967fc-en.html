A Remote-Controlled Desk Robot for a Fun Break

A tiny desk robot controllable via a browser, perfect for a fun and customizable break.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Designed by Akashdeep Singh, this desk robot stands out from autonomous models by offering remote control functionality. It’s primarily a distraction tool, created to provide a playful break without any practical pretension. Its 3D-printable frame houses a Seeed Studio XAIO ESP32-C3 board as its brain, a 1.3-inch OLED screen for displaying facial expressions, and two N20 gear motors for mobility.

Interaction happens via a smartphone: the robot broadcasts a Wi-Fi network that the user connects to. A self-hosted web app, accessible through a browser, allows real-time control of the device. Basic commands (move forward, backward, turn) are complemented by special modes, such as a dance routine. According to the creator, the responsiveness of the controls and the smoothness of the movements make the driving experience particularly enjoyable on a desk.

The project is open source: the 3D design files and code are available on Singh’s Cults3D page. The build relies on accessible components and 3D printing, making it a feasible project for hobbyists and electronics enthusiasts.
