This display module, designed for interactive kiosks and digital signage systems, adopts the SDM-L form factor (Smart Display Module Large), an Intel standard already used in professional applications. Unlike the smaller SDM-S format, SDM-L offers extended connectivity for bandwidth-intensive and feature-rich use cases.

The module connects via a 98-pin PCIe edge connector, a standard for digital signage cards. It includes a HDMI 2.0 port for video output, as well as a 22-pin MIPI DSI/CSI-2 connector for an internal camera or display. Networking features a Gigabit Ethernet RJ45 port, complemented by the Raspberry Pi Compute Module 5’s Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.2.

Available interfaces include two USB 3.0 Type-A ports and a USB 2.0 Type-C port for programming. An M.2 expansion socket allows for additional modules, while I2C, UART, and SPI signals enable integration with sensors or other peripherals. The module targets developers building custom solutions for kiosks, digital signage, or point-of-sale systems.
