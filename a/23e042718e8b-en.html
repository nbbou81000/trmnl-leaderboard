An LCR Meter with Compromises: What You Need to Know

The ZOYI ZT-XC1 LCR meter measures inductance and capacitance, but with practical limitations to consider before purchasing.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

The ZOYI ZT-XC1 is a multimeter that includes inductance (L), capacitance (C), and resistance (R) measurement capabilities, a feature now common in hobbyist labs. According to Kerry Wong, an expert in test instruments, this model stands out for its ability to measure these parameters but comes with notable trade-offs.

The charging port, located at the back of the device, is inconvenient for continuous use. More surprisingly, in standard multimeter mode, the ZT-XC1 cannot measure capacitance or current, limiting its versatility as a general-purpose multimeter. For inductance and capacitance measurements, the manufacturer provides two dedicated adapters to plug into the front of the device: one for through-hole components and another for surface-mount devices (SMD). These adapters simplify measurements but are only suitable for components that fit their size constraints.

Another common limitation of such devices is their difficulty in compensating for the parasitic capacitance of test leads. The ZT-XC1 partially addresses this issue with its dedicated adapters, but they do not accommodate all component formats. Finally, Kerry Wong notes that this model does not offer a frequency response curve for capacitors, a useful feature for characterizing components from unreliable suppliers.
