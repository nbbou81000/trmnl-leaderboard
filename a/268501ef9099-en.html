WeAct Logic Analyzer V2: A $10 USB 3.0 Logic Analyzer Board

A 16-channel, 200 MHz USB 3.0 logic analyzer board compatible with Sigrok, priced at $10.

WeAct Studio has launched the Logic Analyzer V2, a low-cost USB 3.0 logic analyzer board featuring the WCH CH32H417 microcontroller with a RISC-V core running up to 400 MHz. The compact board (42.5 × 26.5 × 13 mm) includes 896KB of SRAM and 960KB of flash, with 304KB reserved for custom user firmware.

It operates as a 16-channel logic analyzer with a bandwidth of up to 200 MHz over USB 3.0, or 40 MHz on 8 channels with USB 2.0. Sampling rates range from 4 to 200 MHz depending on the mode and channel count. The U3LogicAnalyzer tool, a fork of Sigrok/PulseView, enables signal visualization and decoding (UART, I²C, SPI, CAN, etc.).

The board offers three selectable modes via a button: logic analyzer, USB-serial + DAP probe (CMSIS-DAP v2), or custom firmware. A WS2812 LED serves as a status indicator. WeAct provides three open-source tools for the board: U3LogicAnalyzer, CH32H417_LogicAnalyzer_Upgrade (firmware updates), and WeActStudio.DAPFlasher (programming Arm Cortex-M targets).

Available on AliExpress for around $10 (excluding shipping and taxes), the board includes a USB-C port for power and data. The logic analyzer and DAP firmware are distributed as closed-source binaries.
