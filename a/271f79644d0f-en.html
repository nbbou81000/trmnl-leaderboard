Stackable Gridfinity Screw Storage Unit for Small Hardware

A modular and stackable storage system designed to organize screws and small mechanical parts, compatible with the Gridfinity ecosystem.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

This project introduces a Gridfinity storage unit designed for screws and small mechanical parts, designed to fit into a larger organization system. The main model, the 3×5 ScrewUnit, is a compact version with modular drawers and tailored storage options. Two additional variants are available: a small-drawer model (2×5) and a mixed-drawer version (2×5) combining compartments and drawers.

Key features include drawer stops to prevent screws from spilling, a Gridfinity locking system for stable stacking, and scoop or no-scoop drawer options for easy retrieval. A magnetic top is also offered for compatible models, allowing tools or small metal parts to be attached. The units feature large screw holes for wall mounting and are compatible with Rugged Organizer drawers.

The 3D model files are available for download on MakerWorld, with estimated print times provided for each variant. This project is part of a robust and customizable storage solution, ideal for workshops or organized workspaces.
