Build a Star Wars Control Panel with Cardboard Kitbashing

A Lucasfilm engineer shares how to build a Star Wars control panel from cardboard and household scraps, inspired by the Andor series props.

Crew Parker, a mechanical engineer and Lucasfilm asset coordinator, shares a method to build a Star Wars control panel from everyday materials. Inspired by the Andor series props, particularly those in Luthen Rael’s gallery, this project targets model enthusiasts and upcyclers without requiring a 3D printer.

The core technique is kitbashing, which involves assembling repurposed items—bottle caps, lids, plastic scraps—to create intricate visual details. Parker outlines the steps: cutting two base shapes from cardboard, attaching them to a central strip, and painting the panel in gold or bronze. Black lines drawn with markers or paint enhance the realism.

To add texture, the author suggests greebling the panel by gluing circular elements (caps, lids) or small dials (pipette tips, plastic straws). Buttons can be cut from colored paper or replaced with flat thumbtacks. Parker emphasizes customization: colors and materials depend on available resources.

The project can be enhanced with basic electronics: LEDs inserted into the cardboard for illumination, or functional buttons cut carefully to trigger hidden components. The goal remains accessible, even for beginners, using simple tools like a craft knife, hot glue, and paint.
