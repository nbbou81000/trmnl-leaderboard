The Python for Microcontrollers Newsletter is a weekly publication focused on Python developments for embedded hardware, including microcontrollers and single-board computers like the Raspberry Pi. Each issue, delivered ad-free and spam-free, compiles the latest software news, events, projects, and hardware innovations related to CircuitPython, MicroPython, and Python in general.

The content is curated to highlight essential updates that enthusiasts may have missed. The newsletter is sent every Monday at 11 am (US Eastern Time) and is available for free. Subscribers can unsubscribe at any time. Organizers also encourage sharing the newsletter with colleagues, students, or fellow enthusiasts.
