M5Stack Unveils ESP32-S3-Powered ePaper DIY and Pre-Assembled Kits

M5Stack offers two ePaper development kits powered by the ESP32-S3, one DIY and one pre-assembled, featuring a 4.7" display and a built-in battery.

M5Stack has launched two new ePaper development kits, one designed for DIY assembly and the other pre-assembled, both powered by the Espressif ESP32-S3 microcontroller. The ESP32-S3 features a dual-core processor running up to 240 MHz, 8MB of pseudo-static RAM (PSRAM), and 16MB of on-board flash memory.

The PaperDIY Assembly Kit is aimed at hobbyists and includes a main control board, a 4.7" electrophoretic ePaper display (900×540 resolution), a 140mAh lithium battery, and all necessary components for self-assembly. The PaperDIY Complete Kit, on the other hand, comes fully assembled in a 3D-printed enclosure, with the same components inside. The enclosure's source files are provided for customization or 3D printing.

The ePaper display uses electrophoretic technology, which moves physical ink particles to render images and consumes power only during screen updates. This makes it highly energy-efficient, but it lacks a backlight, so visibility is limited to well-lit environments. The refresh rate is slower than traditional displays, and the panel supports only 16 shades of gray. Connectivity is limited to single-band 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, though the ESP32-S3 module includes Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) hardware support, which is not enabled in the default firmware.

The PaperDIY Assembly Kit is available for purchase on the M5Stack store for $25, while the PaperDIY Complete Kit is listed at $29.90 but was out of stock at the time of the announcement.
