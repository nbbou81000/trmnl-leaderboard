Tamagotchi Paradise Hackable via UART for Advanced Tweaks

A $10 UART adapter enables firmware modification and game execution on the 2026 Tamagotchi Paradise.

The Tamagotchi Paradise, the latest in the Tamagotchi virtual pet line, stands out for its hackable design. Unlike earlier models, it features an accessible UART (Universal Asynchronous Receiver-Transmitter) interface via a custom dock, enabling direct communication with its 32-bit ARM microcontroller.

The hack uses an affordable USB-to-UART adapter, the CH340G, housed in a 3D-printed enclosure. Three wires connect the adapter to nickel strips embedded in the case, forming a pogo-style connector that aligns with the Tamagotchi’s contacts. Once plugged in, the adapter emulates the official dock, granting direct access to the device’s internal system.

The possibilities are vast: firmware modification to add new features, running retro games like Super Mario or DOOM, or even browser-based control via a dedicated Google Chrome tool. The project’s creator, [Squalius], demonstrates these capabilities by connecting the adapter to a PC, paving the way for advanced software tweaks. This approach continues the tradition of Tamagotchi hacks, which have long attracted electronics and retro-computing enthusiasts.

The Tamagotchi Paradise joins a long line of retro devices reinvented as creative platforms.
