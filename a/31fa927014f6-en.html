Sixfab AI HAT+ Brings 25 TOPS AI Acceleration at 3W to Raspberry Pi 5

A 25 TOPS NPU accelerator for Raspberry Pi 5, optimized for low-power, always-on local AI systems.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

The Sixfab AI HAT+ is an expansion board for Raspberry Pi 5 featuring a DEEPX DX-M1M NPU, delivering up to 25 TOPS of dedicated AI acceleration. While peak TOPS figures dominate marketing claims, the real breakthrough lies in its power efficiency: around 3 W under sustained load, enabling compact system designs without active cooling.

This NPU is engineered to handle three core AI tasks locally: perception (object detection, segmentation, pose estimation), contextual understanding (compact vision-language models or VLM) and interaction (compact language models or SLM). Pre-optimized models from the DEEPX ModelZoo include YOLO-based detectors and tools like DX-Stream to assemble GStreamer-based video pipelines.

The DX-M1M’s efficiency stems from 2 GB of dedicated LPDDR4X memory, sufficient to run vision and language models simultaneously. DEEPX prioritizes INT8 model optimization using real-world deployment data, not idealized test images, to ensure accuracy in challenging conditions (low light, motion blur, occlusion).

Raspberry Pi OS simplifies setup with commands to verify hardware, run a pre-packaged example, and monitor NPU activity in real time. Developers can then use ModelZoo’s optimized models or import custom ONNX models via DX-COM. Local applications, such as a 3D print failure detector or a home security camera, highlight targeted use cases: low latency, privacy, and energy autonomy.
