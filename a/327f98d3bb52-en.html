Turning a Pixel Watch into a Mini Smart Home Dashboard

An old Pixel Watch can double as a compact smart home dashboard, no subscription or dedicated screen required.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

A smart home dashboard doesn’t always need a large rectangular screen. An old Pixel Watch offers a compact alternative, with a display under 2 inches, perfect for a secondary control point in your home. Unlike tablets or dedicated screens, this approach leverages Wear OS apps, which are already designed for minimalist interfaces and simplified touch interactions.

The author configured their Pixel Watch as a dashboard using Google Home, SmartThings, and other services. By selecting the "Active" watch face with a "Minimal" layout, they added complications like weather, smart home scenes, or the Gemini assistant. Once paired, the watch operates independently, even without a connected phone, though Google restricts using multiple active watches simultaneously in the Health app.

For a fixed setup, the author 3D-printed a stand to place the watch near the bed, making it easy to control lights and scenes without relying on a smartphone or smart speaker. Another idea is to create a dock that incorporates the charger, turning the watch into a portable remote. Older Pixel Watch models, with their inductive charging, work better for this than newer side-charging versions.

This solution complements larger dashboards but prevents an unused smartwatch from gathering dust. Despite their low trade-in value, Pixel Watches can find a second life as a compact, discreet control tool.
