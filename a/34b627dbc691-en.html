Seeed Studio Launches XIAO Board with STM32C5A3CG MCU

Seeed Studio integrates an STM32 microcontroller for the first time on a XIAO board, featuring CAN FD and a heated IMU.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Seeed Studio expands its XIAO development board lineup with a groundbreaking model: the first to feature an STM32 microcontroller. The XIAO STM32C5 integrates the STM32C5A3CG chip, built around an Arm Cortex-M33 core clocked up to 144 MHz, with hardware acceleration for floating-point operations and digital signal processing (DSP).

Despite its ultra-compact form factor (barely larger than a thumbnail), the board packs advanced features. It includes two native CAN FD controllers for industrial and automotive communications, one paired with a Texas Instruments TCAN3403-Q1 transceiver, while the second is left available for an external transceiver. An additional 16MB of external flash memory complements the 256KB of SRAM and 1MB of on-chip flash.

A six-axis IMU (Inertial Measurement Unit) is onboard, enhancing motion tracking accuracy through programmable heating of internal resistors before measurements. Connectivity is handled via a USB Type-C port for both power and data.

Priced at $9.90 or $10.90 depending on whether I/O headers are pre-soldered, the board supports STM32Cube IDE, MicroPython, and the Zephyr RTOS via PlatformIO. Additional documentation is available on Seeed Studio’s wiki.
