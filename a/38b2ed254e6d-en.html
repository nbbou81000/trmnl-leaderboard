3 Spooky Home Assistant Projects for Halloween

Home Assistant automations to scare trick-or-treaters: doorbell, lights, and animatronic skeleton.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

For Halloween, three Home Assistant automations aim to spook trick-or-treaters. The first uses a video doorbell camera (such as Reolink) to trigger eerie audio messages when a person is detected. Facial recognition and pre-recorded (or text-to-speech generated) voices play threatening phrases or sinister laughter. An outdoor Philips Hue light, set to dark colors, enhances the effect.

The second project turns RGB smart bulbs into haunted house elements. A Home Assistant script offers four lighting effects: flickering red flames, dramatic white flashes, a dim green-blue glow, or a transition between orange, purple, and green. Users can adjust timing, brightness, and colors. Once configured, activating the script makes the lights animate automatically.

The final project features an animatronic skeleton controlled by an ESP8266 board. Its skull, mounted on a servo motor, turns to face passersby. RGB LEDs mimic blinking and changing eyes, while an MP3 module plays pre-recorded sounds (laughter, growls). A motion sensor triggers the action, and the setup can be controlled via MQTT from Home Assistant. Full instructions, parts list, and custom firmware are available online for replication or customization.
