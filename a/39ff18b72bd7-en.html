Adafruit: DIY HomeView, Metro ESP32-P4 Case and Pine Cone LED Lantern

Pedro showcases a DIY replica of Apple HomeView, Noe prototypes a snap-fit case for the Metro ESP32-P4, and a pine cone LED lantern model is highlighted.

In a recent live stream, Pedro from Adafruit showcased a DIY version of the Apple HomeView, a customizable home dashboard. This open-source project allows displaying information like weather or connected device status on a touchscreen.

Noe also unveiled a prototype of a snap-fit case for the Metro ESP32-P4, a development board based on the ESP32-P4 microcontroller. The case, designed for tool-free assembly, simplifies integrating the board into electronic projects.

A pine cone LED lantern model, designed by Animaag, was featured in the Timelapse Tuesday segment. This project combines 3D printing and LED lighting to create a festive decoration. The 3D files are available on MakerWorld and YouTube.

Links to the mentioned products, such as the Metro ESP32-P4 and the 7-inch and 5-inch touchscreens, are accessible on Adafruit’s website. The community is encouraged to share their own projects combining 3D printing and electronics via the Adafruit Learning System.
