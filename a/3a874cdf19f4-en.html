Six Arduino Projects for Halloween 2026: Robots, Pumpkins and Automata

From smart pumpkins to terrifying robots, six Arduino projects for a geeky and connected Halloween.

As Halloween approaches, Arduino presents six inspiring projects for DIY creations blending electronics, coding, and festive spirit. The first, a smart pumpkin, uses a webcam and an onboard vision-language model (VLM) on an Arduino to interact with visitors. Operating offline, it answers voice questions about what it sees, powered by a VENTUNO Q chip.

Another project syncs two store-bought animatronics—a punk skeleton and a reaper—around a Bluetooth speaker. The hack cleverly repurposes the existing analog signal to trigger movements without altering the devices. For beginners, an ultrasonic spider drops onto passersby within 40 cm using a servo and ultrasonic sensor, with an automatic reset mechanism.

A step-by-step guide shows how to build a cardboard Portal sentry turret replica with an Arduino ATtiny85, featuring a blinking LED and integrated audio. More ambitious, a T-1-7 warrior robot, inspired by the Terminator universe, moves via an electric wheelchair base and includes radio controls, lights, and sound effects. Finally, the UNO R4 Plug and Make kit enables spooky light and sound effects triggered by a distance sensor, with remote control options via Arduino Cloud.
