10 Wild Projects Not to Miss at Maker Faire Orlando 2026

A private jet on a Prius, word portraits, an AI in a pickup… Here are 10 gems from the 2026 edition.

The 2026 edition of Maker Faire Orlando promises as many wild as varied projects. Among the first announcements, a private jet turned into a hybrid car stands out: Steve Woodruff grafted the fuselage of a 1982 British Aerospace Hawker onto the chassis of a 2004 Toyota Prius, creating a drivable limousine where the cockpit, flight controls, and wiring remain visible. The vehicle is street-legal and complies with legal width and height limits.

Another highlight is Freedom A.I. by John Bach, a customizable assistant whose voice, personality, and interface adapt to user needs. Bach will showcase his assistant « Ghost », integrated into a modified Chevy Silverado, capable of interacting with visitors via speakers and a physical animated character.

Autonomous inventions and artistic creations

John Smith will exhibit Rocket Tracker, an autonomous rocket-tracking system. Equipped with a joystick to steer a gimbal holding two cameras, it automatically locks onto a launcher as soon as it’s detected, allowing to film the rocket’s trajectory and plume, visible over 140 miles away in clear weather.

Curtis Epperson presents artistic portraits where each image is made of hundreds of hand-drawn words and phrases, with no repetition. His works, seemingly classic at first glance, reveal their complexity up close. Jacob Bates pushes photography art further by designing his own cameras using 3D printing and open-source designs.

The Astro Crow studio will showcase Jai-Alai Heroes, a retro arcade game entirely designed and manufactured locally, dedicated to this typical Florida sport. Nearby, Jupiter 7 will turn a booth into a space workshop where laser cutters and 3D printers will let visitors build their own scientific creations.

The Orlando Ghostbusters will unveil their latest creations, with 3D-printed props and fabrication demonstrations. Warren Hart III, known as Famous After Death, will present his original prints, made from digitally designed and 3D-printed printing blocks.
