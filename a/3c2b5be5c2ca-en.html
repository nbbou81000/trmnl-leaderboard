Toshiba Launches 120MHz TXZ+ M4H Microcontrollers for Entry-Level Applications

Toshiba releases a new line of 120MHz Cortex-M4 microcontrollers designed for basic system control applications.

Toshiba has begun mass production of its TXZ+ M4H microcontroller family, targeting system control applications in consumer products and industrial equipment. The manufacturer states these chips are designed for systems requiring real-time performance, operational stability, and modular design, while remaining adaptable to future updates.

The microcontrollers feature an Arm Cortex-M4F core running at up to 120 MHz, providing sufficient processing power for basic control tasks, according to Toshiba. They include 18KB of SRAM with parity protection and 256KB of flash memory supporting up to 100,000 program/erase cycles. Peripherals include three or four UART interfaces, an I2C bus, a TSPI controller, a 12-bit ADC, and a dedicated CRC32/CRC16 circuit.

Available models offer up to 51 general-purpose input/output (GPIO) pins, a 27- or 32-channel DMA controller, and external interrupts on 11, 12, or 16 pins, depending on the variant. Three models are already available through Farnell: TMPM4H4FYUG, TMPM4H1FYUG, and TMPM4H2FYDUG.
