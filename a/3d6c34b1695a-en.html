Tektronix Launches Low-Cost DPO Oscilloscopes Starting at $450 with AI and Touchscreen

Tektronix introduces a line of budget digital oscilloscopes featuring a 7-inch touchscreen, 12-bit resolution, and AI-assisted control starting at $450.

Tektronix, traditionally known for high-end oscilloscopes, has launched the 1 Series DPO, a range of budget digital phosphor oscilloscopes designed for electronics testing, embedded development, and education. These devices feature a 7-inch capacitive touchscreen (1024 x 600 resolution) and support up to four analog channels.

The entry-level models, such as the DPO1052, offer a 50 MHz bandwidth and 12-bit vertical resolution. The sampling rate reaches 1.25 GS/s with two active channels, and 625 MS/s with four. Each channel provides 10 million points of memory, while the waveform capture rate is approximately 125,000 waveforms per second. Inputs accept sensitivities from 1 mV/div to 10 V/div, with an impedance of 1 MΩ and a typical capacitance of 17 pF.

Built-in tools include a 5-digit digital voltmeter, a 6-digit frequency counter, and advanced analysis features: FFT, waveform math, limit testing, and XY plots. The S models add a 25 MHz arbitrary function generator (sine, square, ramp, etc.) and frequency response analysis (Bode plot).

For embedded development, the oscilloscope supports I2C, SPI, and UART protocol triggering and decoding, displaying decoded data alongside captured signals. An Ethernet 10/100 Mbps interface enables remote control via the eScope tool. Tektronix also offers an early-access Tek Intelligence connector for AI-assisted control using natural-language instructions.

The 16 available variants differ in bandwidth (50 MHz to 200 MHz), channel count (2 or 4), and the presence of the function generator. Prices start at $450 for the DPO1052, while the top-end DPO1204S (200 MHz, 4 channels, built-in generator) is priced at $1,650.
