A Foldable Retro Arcade Cabinet Fits in a Wooden Briefcase

A maker built a portable two-player arcade cabinet that folds like a briefcase, featuring an LCD screen and professional-grade controls.

Maker Michael Alm has created a portable two-player arcade cabinet that folds up like a wooden briefcase. When opened, the upper half features a 15.6-inch LCD screen, while the lower half houses two sets of arcade controls. Closing the cabinet folds the screen over the controls, and a carrying handle makes it easy to transport.

The system runs on a Raspberry Pi 3 Model B+ powered by Batocera, a Linux-based OS designed for retro gaming and emulation. This setup grants access to thousands of classic games. The controls use professional-grade Sanwa joysticks and Sanwa arcade buttons arranged in the Vewlix layout for optimal finger positioning. The control panel is tilted at 7.5 degrees for comfort, with additional buttons for inserting virtual coins, starting games, and navigating menus.

An I-PAC 2 controller interface connects the joysticks and buttons to the Raspberry Pi, converting inputs into signals the computer can recognize without custom software. The enclosure is built from Baltic birch plywood, cut with a laser cutter and routed to secure the arcade buttons. A custom hinge system made from brass tubing and rods allows the cabinet to fold without the joysticks hitting the screen.

This project blends nostalgia with portability, delivering a modular retro gaming experience that’s easy to take anywhere.
