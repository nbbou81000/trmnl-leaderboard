VIEWE Launches ESP32-S31 Development Board with 4.3-inch Display

First third-party board based on ESP32-S31, featuring a 4.3-inch display, Wi-Fi 6, and multiple interfaces.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

VIEWE has unveiled the PLCM-UES31S043H800V480C-U, a development board featuring a 4.3-inch display and the new ESP32-S31 microcontroller. This module stands out as one of the first third-party solutions to utilize this still-preview processor. The 800 × 480 IPS TFT screen, connected via an ST7262E43-G4 controller, delivers 16.2 million colors and 400 cd/m² brightness, paired with a capacitive touch panel (GT911).

The board integrates an ESP32-S31-WROOM-3-N16R16V module with Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax), Bluetooth 5.4 (including LE Audio and Bluetooth Mesh 1.1), and an 802.15.4 radio for Zigbee, Thread, and Matter. It also includes USB-C and USB-A ports, a microSD slot, RS485 and CAN interfaces, and a 20-pin GPIO header with UART and I2C. Audio features include an ES8389 codec, two NS4150B amplifiers, two onboard microphones, and a buzzer.

Powered by 5V, the board operates within a -20°C to +70°C temperature range. Its ESP32-S31 processor, based on a RISC-V architecture, combines a high-performance core (320 MHz) and a low-power core. Memory includes 512 KB SRAM, 32 KB low-power SRAM, 16 MB PSRAM, and 16 MB flash. Development is done via the ESP-IDF framework in master branch with the --preview flag, while VIEWE provides examples (LCD, audio, microSD, RS485/CAN interfaces) and an LVGL interface on GitHub. Linux compatibility may be possible thanks to an ESP32-S31 BSP released by Espressif.

Available for $38.99 on VIEWE’s store, the board is expected to be listed on AliExpress in the coming days.
