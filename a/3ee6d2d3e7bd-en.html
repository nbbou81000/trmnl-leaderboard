A 3D-printed enclosure is designed to hold a Raspberry Pi 5 alongside its official AI HAT, featuring an integrated camera mount at the top. This all-in-one setup simplifies embedded AI projects, such as computer vision pipelines, by providing a compact and organized workspace for on-device machine learning experiments.

The design is compatible with popular desktop 3D printers, including the Bambu A1 mini, P1S, and X1 Carbon. Aimed at makers and developers exploring local AI applications, the case ensures efficient space usage while maintaining a sturdy structure for AI-driven experiments on microcomputers.
