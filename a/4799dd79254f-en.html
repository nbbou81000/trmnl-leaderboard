Text Adventure Game on an E-Paper Handheld Powered by ESP32

A DIY project turns an e-paper screen into a portable console for text adventure games, powered by WiFi and an ESP32.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Jeremy Ngai has built a portable device designed for text adventure games, leveraging the benefits of a 2.9-inch e-paper screen. The handheld combines a rotary encoder for navigation, an RGB LED for ambient lighting, and a battery providing up to 20 hours of runtime. The device connects to WiFi via a captive portal, similar to other ESP32-based projects, before fetching dynamically generated stories from the Gemini service.

The project features a custom PCB and a 3D-printed enclosure, giving it a polished look. Once the story is loaded, users navigate choices using the rotary encoder, while the RGB LED adjusts its lighting to match the story’s mood. The full source code is available on GitHub, allowing anyone to replicate or modify the device.

Commenters praise the match between e-paper and text-based games, though some note the lack of a full keyboard limits compatibility with complex titles like those in the Z-machine format. Others argue the screen, while readable, may be too small for an optimal experience.
