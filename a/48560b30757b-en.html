A Free Cloud AI Agent Hosted on Your Own Cloudflare Account

A personal AI assistant running in your free Cloudflare account, with no telemetry and open-source code on GitHub.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Talorys, created by rociiu, offers a personal AI agent hosted directly in your Cloudflare account, avoiding reliance on opaque cloud services. Unlike traditional solutions, the tool leverages Cloudflare’s free tier with reasonable usage limits for personal tasks. The code, available on GitHub, is fully inspectable, ensuring complete transparency about its operations.

The agent functions as a task manager, reminder system, and note-taking tool, without aiming to replace general-purpose chatbots. It cannot integrate with external calendars, process emails, perform web searches, or generate code. Its scope is limited to organizing lists and storing information, with the ability to easily review or delete data stored in your Cloudflare instance.

Installation is straightforward: connect a custom domain or use the subdomain provided by Cloudflare, then run a single command on your machine. The system automatically handles authentication and service configuration. However, technical limitations remain: scheduling errors (such as misplaced meetings) and rate-limiting issues tied to Cloudflare’s daily quotas were observed during testing. Despite these flaws, the tool highlights the potential of free cloud resources for lightweight projects, as noted by Hackaday in a recent article about Oracle’s free servers.
