Learn Japanese with a Portable E-Ink Display

An open-source project turns an e-ink screen into a Japanese learning assistant, featuring flashcards and progress tracking.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Mastering a language like Japanese relies heavily on repetition and immersion. Instead of carrying physical flashcards, Enkiplate offers a digital alternative using the Inkplate 4Tempera e-ink screen. This portable device helps users learn vocabulary and sentences while tracking progress, starting with 12 cards per day.

The system also displays calendar events and weather updates via an online service. It begins with kana—the two Japanese phonetic writing systems—before gradually introducing kanji and sentences. However, the built-in ESP32-WROVER-E limits local storage to 16 cards at a time, requiring a PC application to add new cards.
