Maker Faire Orlando 2026: Retro Games, Robots, and DIY Tech

Maker Faire Orlando 2026 showcases retro gaming, robots, and DIY projects in a uniquely creative Florida atmosphere.

Every November, Orlando hosts Maker Faire, an event where local engineers, artists, and tinkerers showcase their creations. The 2026 edition will highlight projects blending technological nostalgia with innovation, with a strong focus on retro gaming and DIY electronics.

Retro gaming and reimagined consoles

Several exhibits reflect this trend, such as HackArcade, a Tampa Hackerspace project featuring restored or homebuilt arcade cabinets, including a homemade claw machine. PiPlay, developed by Shea Silverman, offers consoles based on Raspberry Pi, Game Boy Advance, or NES, along with tools to build your own systems. Meanwhile, the Central Florida Retro Computer Club displays functional machines from the 1970s to 1990s, including TRS-80s, Commodore 64s, and Apple IIs, as well as homebrew projects using 8-bit processors like the 6502 or Z80.

A highlight will be the talk by Mark Kounalis from MakerFX Makerspace on the retro computing community, scheduled for Saturday from 11:30 am to 12:15 pm.

Games and interactive experiences

The event also features original creations inspired by retro gaming. Jai-Alai Heroes, developed by Astro Crow, is an exclusive arcade game blending fast-paced sport with 1980s-1990s pixel art. Invaders from Planet Makey! turns a cow-rescue mission into an interactive game, complete with a motorized ball cannon, light and sound effects, and an automatic projectile recovery system.

Another standout project is MCParks, a 1:1 scale Minecraft recreation of famous theme parks, built using satellite imagery, architectural blueprints, and 3D models. The project, in development for over 13 years, even includes functional rides and shows.

In cosplay, Maker Faire Orlando celebrates extreme craftsmanship with costumes inspired by franchises like Star Wars, Doctor Who, or Fallout, combining 3D printing, foam sculpting, electronics, and sewing.
