Home Assistant Renames Its Cloud Service to Home Assistant Link

Home Assistant’s online service is renamed to better reflect its values: private, optional, and without vendor lock-in.

Nabu Casa, the commercial arm behind Home Assistant, is renaming its cloud service to Home Assistant Link to better align with its core values: privacy, optionality, and freedom from vendor lock-in. The move addresses criticisms of traditional cloud services, which often feature rising subscription costs, outages, and data monetization.

Launched in 2018 to sustain Home Assistant’s development, the service originally provided remote access to home automation without complex setup. Today, it funds the Open Home Foundation, a nonprofit focused on privacy and open-source sustainability. Unlike Big Tech offerings, Home Assistant Link remains optional: the system operates locally even if the subscription lapses.

The service emphasizes technical and ethical distinctions. User data is encrypted and inaccessible to Nabu Casa or the foundation, preventing commercial exploitation. Online features like speech-to-text or cloud backups prioritize reliability and speed while remaining compatible with self-hosted alternatives. A streamlined interface and access management tools have also been introduced to enhance usability.

Home Assistant Link is now available to existing and new subscribers. The service continues to evolve, with regular backend updates to boost performance and security.
