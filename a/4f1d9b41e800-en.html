Emulate a 486 PC Directly in Your Browser

A 100% web-based emulator recreates a full Intel 486DX2-66 PC, including BIOS, VGA, and retro games.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Jan de Jager has created a browser-based emulator for an Intel 486DX2-66 PC, running entirely within a single tab without requiring any downloads. The emulator doesn’t just simulate the CPU—it also emulates the motherboard and ISA/VLB adapter cards, providing a near-authentic experience of 1990s hardware.

Built in ES6 JavaScript, the emulator supports classic software like SeaBIOS, the VGA BIOS, and FreeDOS 1.3. It can run retro games such as Wolfenstein 3D, Duke Nukem, and DOOM, as well as other DOS applications. A built-in control panel lets users tweak hardware settings, including storage, expansion cards, and memory.

This project leverages the raw power of modern browsers, which can now emulate complex architectures without dedicated hardware. Unlike traditional emulators that required local installation, this solution runs entirely in the browser, highlighting how retro-computing tools have evolved with web technologies.
