LineageOS: Pure Android on Smartphones and Raspberry Pi

LineageOS provides an open Android version, free from bloatware and restrictions, compatible with smartphones and Raspberry Pi.

LineageOS is a fully open-source Android distribution maintained by a community, derived from CyanogenMod and based on the Android Open Source Project (AOSP). Unlike manufacturers' proprietary versions, it allows free customization of the system, enabling developer mode without restrictions and removing unwanted apps. LineageOS supports many devices, including older smartphones, and provides over-the-air (OTA) updates for the same major version.

Installing LineageOS on smartphones can be complex due to hardware locks imposed by manufacturers. For example, a 2016 Xiaomi Mi 5, stuck on Android 8.0, was upgraded to Android 15 via LineageOS after officially unlocking the bootloader. However, the process requires tools like Fastboot and ADB, and may need specific drivers, such as the one provided by the official Xiaomi Mi Unlock Tool. Steps include flashing a recovery image, then installing LineageOS via ADB.

LineageOS is also available for boards like the Raspberry Pi 4, thanks to unofficial ports maintained by Konga. These versions, available in standard Android or Android TV, offer an optimized interface and immediate hardware compatibility, including video decoding. Installation is as simple as writing the image to an SD card, with no complex steps. Versions for Raspberry Pi 3 and 5 are also available.
