Luxonis OAK 4 FFC: A Modular Board for Up to Five Cameras in Robotics

Luxonis unveils the OAK 4 FFC, a modular board supporting up to five cameras for robotics projects.

Computer vision company Luxonis has expanded its OAK Modular lineup with the OAK 4 FFC, a modular board designed to support up to five cameras simultaneously. This solution targets developers building humanoid robots, drones, or medical systems, allowing them to select and position sensors based on their project’s needs rather than adapting the robot to a fixed camera setup. According to Luxonis CEO Bradley Dillon, this approach addresses a common demand: « The machines our customers are building don’t have room for a camera designed for someone else’s machine. They want to choose their sensors and their placement, and we handle the rest. »

The system is powered by the OAK 4 system-on-module (SoM), an Arm-based six-core chip paired with an integrated GPU. The SoM delivers 4 TOPS (tera operations per second) of FP16 compute, supplemented by a dedicated neural coprocessor offering 12 TOPS in FP16 or 48 TOPS in INT8 for on-device neural network execution. The OAK 4 FFC carrier board features five FFC (Flat Flexible Circuit) connectors compatible with four-lane MIPI CSI interfaces, supporting three sensor options: the Sony IMX586 (48MP, color, rolling shutter), the Omnivision OV9828 (1MP, monochrome, global shutter), or the Omnivision OG05 (5MP, monochrome or color, global shutter).

The board also includes advanced features like stereo depth generation, feature tracking, and a neural network interface. Connectivity options include a 2.5G Ethernet port (Hirose M12 connector), a USB Type-C port (up to 5 Gb/s in host or device mode), and interfaces such as SPI, I2C, UART, I2S, CAN, and GPIO. Local storage is provided via an SD card slot, with an optional add-on enabling PoE+ (Power over Ethernet Plus) support. An onboard six-axis IMU (Inertial Measurement Unit) and a separate three-axis magnetometer are included for motion tracking.

Two variants are available at launch: the OAK-4-FFC-BAREBONE (bare carrier board with OAK 4 module) priced at $729, and the OAK-4-FFC-DEVKIT (complete kit with mounting hardware and heatsink) at $829. Luxonis notes a « temporary $200 surcharge for memory and storage. » A reference design for the carrier board is also released under an open hardware license.
