Rotary Encoder with 1.8-inch Touch Display Upgraded to ESP32-S31 SoC for $40

A rotary encoder with a 1.8-inch round touchscreen display upgrades to ESP32-S31, featuring WiFi 6, Bluetooth 5.4, and a built-in speaker for $40 on AliExpress.

Manufacturer GUITION has released an updated version of its JC3636F118C-I-YR1 rotary encoder, now powered by an ESP32-S31NRV16 microcontroller. This SoC, based on the RISC-V architecture, features a high-performance core running at 320 MHz, a FPU, and multimedia accelerators for decoding MJPEG streams and accelerating 2D graphics.

The device includes a 1.8-inch round touchscreen display with 262,000 colors at a 360×360 resolution, driven by an ST77916 controller. A PCM5100APWR speaker and MSM261D4030H1CPM MEMS microphone are built-in, while the rotary encoder offers haptic feedback via a DRV2605LDGSR circuit. The module also includes 16MB of PSRAM, 16MB of SPI flash, and a microSD card slot for storing WiFi configurations, music, or videos.

Connectivity options include WiFi 6 (802.11ax), Bluetooth 5.4 (with LE Audio and direction finding support), and an 802.15.4 module for Zigbee, Thread, and Matter protocols. Power is delivered via a USB-C port, with an optional rechargeable battery (capacity unspecified). Power consumption is rated at 1.3W.

The preloaded firmware includes features like a clock, countdown timer, audio player, MJPEG/AVI video player, photo frame, spectrum analyzer, and USB HID control. Secondary development is supported via Arduino IDE, ESP-IDF, MicroPython, or GUITION’s proprietary environment, though only ESP-IDF examples are currently provided.

The JC3636F118C-I-YR1 is available for $40.09 with battery and $38.49 without battery on AliExpress, compared to $39.13 for the previous ESP32-S3 version.
