An AI-Generated Electronic Candle in Real Time

An LED candle animated by a tiny neural network generates a realistic and unique flame without repetition.

Traditional LED candles often suffer from a major flaw: their flickering flame follows repetitive, unnatural patterns. To address this, maker Kevin Bates, known for creating the Arduboy handheld gaming console, has developed the Digital Candle, an electronic candle whose flame is generated in real time by artificial intelligence.

Unlike existing solutions that rely on pre-recorded animations, this device uses a convolutional neural network (a lightweight AI model) trained on videos of a real candle. The system continuously analyzes variations in the flame's shape and brightness, then translates them into light patterns displayed on a 144-LED amber matrix (9×16). Each LED can display 256 brightness levels, controlled by an IS31FL3731 driver and an Espressif ESP32-S3 microcontroller. The entire assembly, powered via USB-C, is designed to stand vertically on a dedicated dock.

The system's core relies on a minimalist AI model: a network of 3,545 parameters, combined with a stochastic model of eight damped oscillators, generates unique flame behaviors for each device. Thanks to the unique hardware address of the ESP32-S3, no two candles will ever produce the same animation. The firmware is reprogrammable via USB-C, allowing the matrix to be repurposed as a versatile display (clock, notifications, games like Conway’s Game of Life or Breakout).

Available in black or white, the Digital Candle is priced at $149. While its cost exceeds that of a traditional candle, it appeals to those seeking an innovative approach, endless variability, and customization options via Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Low Energy.
