3D-Printed Handheld Atari 2600 with CRT Screen

A miniature Atari 2600 housed in a 3D-printed case, featuring a repurposed flat CRT screen and USB-C power input.

Nick Bild, creator of miniaturized retro gaming projects, unveils the µTari Go, a portable take on the classic 1970s Atari 2600. This 3D-printed case houses the console’s original circuit board, shrunk down to fit a compact form factor, with buttons arranged like a Game Boy and a cartridge slot above the screen.

The display is a repurposed flat CRT module originally found in devices like Sony’s Watchman or camcorder viewfinders. These modules, still available cheaply on platforms like AliExpress, offer a nostalgic alternative to modern LCD screens. Unlike the original battery-powered models, the µTari Go draws power from an external 12 V source, compatible with USB-C power banks for portable use.

The project highlights the technical quirks of flat CRT displays, including their low power consumption and mechanical simplicity compared to color screens. Commenters discuss the historical significance of abandoned technologies like Sony’s Indextron system, which used a single electron gun to display colors via phosphor stripes.
