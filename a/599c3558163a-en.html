3D-Printed Low Poly Skull Mask for Electronic Halloween

A customizable 3D-printed low poly skull mask, quick to assemble for an instant Halloween costume.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

For an instant Halloween costume, a face mask is a quick solution. MakingThingsCLT shares a 3D-printable low poly skull mask on Printables, designed from a human skull scan sourced from the NIH 3D Print database. The model was manually adjusted in Fusion 360 to refine the polygons, then thickened in Meshmixer, as this step proved problematic in Fusion. The result is a mask with sharp, low poly aesthetics.

The mask fits reasonably well on the face, though it feels slightly tight around the cheeks. It is not compatible with glasses. To secure it, simply add an elastic band or a strip of cloth threaded through the back. The file is available for free download on Printables.
