Ermine: A Versatile Platform for Automotive and Industrial Electronics Hacking

An open-source ESP32-based board to replace, emulate or automate automotive and industrial electronics systems.

St11 Digital introduces Ermine, a modular platform designed to simplify electronics modification projects in vehicles and industrial settings. Born from real-world needs—such as replacing a 1990s Jaguar’s phone system or emulating a CD changer in a 2000s Volvo—this compact and rugged board addresses a gap in the market by offering a ready-to-install solution.

At its core, Ermine features an ESP32 microcontroller with 4 MB of flash memory, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth. The board stands out with its dedicated interfaces: CAN, ISO9141 K-line, LIN, and RS-485, enabling communication with most automotive and industrial electronic systems. It also includes general-purpose digital I/O, a microSD card slot, and a 25-pin D-sub connector for expansions. Two versions are planned: the Ermine XK, with one expansion slot and four high-side MOSFET switches for controlling electrical loads, and the Ermine DX, featuring two expansion slots. Optional modules expand functionality further, including an audio module with stereo DAC and microphone preamplifier, a GNSS module for satellite positioning and a 3-axis gyroscope, and a general-purpose I/O expansion module with four connections.

Built for permanent use, Ermine is housed in a 95×63×25 mm anodized aluminum enclosure. It can be powered by either 5 V or 10 V to 28 V inputs and includes built-in voltage and current monitoring. The firmware runs on an enhanced version of MicroPython, while a self-hosted web application, Ermine Dash, provides programming, file management, interactive terminal access, and data logging. St11 Digital plans to release the project as open source, including hardware schematics, module documentation, firmware, and software tools, once production begins. No launch date or pricing has been announced yet, but interested developers can sign up to receive notifications when the crowdfunding campaign goes live.
