HPMicro Unveils High-Performance RISC-V Analog MCUs with Motor Control

HPMicro launches the HPM5100 series, 32-bit RISC-V MCUs featuring four 16-bit ADCs, op-amps, and PWM controllers for analog sensing and motor control.

HPMicro has expanded its RISC-V microcontroller lineup with the HPM5100 series, tailored for applications requiring both digital processing and advanced analog signal management. The flagship HPM5151 model features a 32-bit RISC-V core running at 400 MHz, with a high-performance mode extending to 480 MHz when powered externally. The chip includes 416 KB of SRAM, 1 MB of flash memory, and dedicated analog peripherals: four 16-bit analog-to-digital converters (ADCs) capable of sampling up to 2 million samples per second (MSPS), configurable to 12-bit resolution at 4 MSPS, along with four operational amplifiers (op-amps) configurable as programmable gain amplifiers (PGA).

,

For motor control, the HPM5151 offers 32 PWM channels across four 8-channel modules. It also integrates a CAN FD controller, a high-speed USB 2.0 OTG interface with built-in PHY, and multiple communication interfaces (UART, SPI, I2C, I2S). The MCU supports hardware floating-point operations and DSP instructions, easing the burden on the CPU for computationally intensive tasks. The HPM5100 series is available in multiple packages: LQFP100 (14×14 mm), QFN68 (8×8 mm), QFN48 (6×6 mm), and QFN32 (5×5 mm), with an operating temperature range from -40°C to +105°C.

Development tools and availability

HPMicro provides an open-source SDK featuring low-level drivers, middleware components like FreeRTOS and TinyUSB, and application examples. SDK version 1.13.0, released in September 2026, officially supports the HPM5100 MCUs and the HPM5100EVK evaluation board. The board includes an HPM5151 MCU, external QSPI NOR flash, an ES8389 audio codec, a CAN transceiver, and an onboard HPM5301 debugger supporting JTAG and serial communication. It features two USB Type-C ports (debugging and OTG), dedicated ADC and op-amp connectors, and a Raspberry Pi-compatible 40-pin expansion header.
