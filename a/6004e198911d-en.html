100% 3D-Printed Bench Vise – No Hardware Required, 100mm Clamping Range

A fully 3D-printed bench vise with no screws or bolts, offering a 100mm clamping range for light-to-medium tasks.

This bench vise model stands out for its fully 3D-printed construction. Measuring 160×160×80 mm with a 100 mm clamping range, it suits light-to-medium tasks like gluing, soldering, or assembling small objects. No metal parts are required—no screws, bolts, or rods are needed. The integrated worm gear mechanism simplifies both manufacturing and use.

The STL file, available for download, requires supports during printing to maintain the spindle hole structure. After printing, sanding the rough area around the hole ensures smooth operation. This project aligns with the DIY tool trend, where 3D printing replaces traditional components.

The files are available on MakerWorld, a platform for community-shared 3D models. This project highlights the shift toward more accessible and customizable DIY tools.
