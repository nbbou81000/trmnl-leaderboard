Three Tools to Build Interactive 3D Floorplans with Home Assistant

Open-source or free tools to turn a 3D model or floorplan image into an interactive smart home dashboard.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Three recent projects make it easier to build interactive 3D smart home dashboards for Home Assistant, each with a different approach. Cabane lets users design a virtual replica of their home, room by room, placing furniture and connected devices. The tool offers first-person navigation (WASD + mouse) and syncs interactions with Home Assistant entities, such as turning on a light. Available as a web version or via an HACS integration, it may soon become open source, according to its creator, who used AI tools during development.

NextFloor generates a 3D floorplan from a drawn layout, featuring a wireframe style. The integration includes predefined assets (IKEA furniture, cars with charge levels) and dynamic features like weather display, heat maps, or motion tracking. Developed partly with AI, the project is open source and still in early stages.

HA Floorplan Card takes a different route: users upload an existing floorplan image (created in another app) and drag-and-drop Home Assistant entities onto it. Unlike the other tools, it doesn’t offer a 3D interactive view but allows visualizing light spread or custom zones. The code, mostly generated by an AI, is available as open source.
