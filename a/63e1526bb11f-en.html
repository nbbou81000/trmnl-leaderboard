3D Microscope for Electronics Inspection with Polarized Lighting

A modular USB microscope offers a rotatable side view for solder inspection with polarized lighting and adjustable optics up to 200x.

For precision electronics work, a top-down view isn’t always enough. [Corebb] built a USB microscope that combines a commercial C-mount lens assembly with a camera, adding a rotatable side-angle view. The lens offers variable magnification up to 200x, ideal for inspecting solder joints or tiny components.

The lighting setup uses an LED ring paired with two polarizing filters: one mounted on the lens and the other on the light source. When positioned at 90 degrees to each other, they eliminate glare from reflective surfaces, enhancing detail visibility.

A 3D-printed adapter, later machined in aluminum, redirects light from the side using two mirrors. A concave lens compensates for the extended optical path, while ball bearings allow smooth rotation around the object. The adapter snaps onto the microscope with magnets, ensuring consistent optical alignment.

Unlike some commercial alternatives, this design relies on accessible components and modular construction. Comments highlight the effectiveness of polarized lighting, often overlooked but essential for such inspections.
