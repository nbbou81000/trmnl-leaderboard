DomeCtrl Launches Crowdfunding for Raspberry Pi 5-Powered Underfloor Heating Controller

An open-source controller to manage underfloor heating via Home Assistant, powered by a Raspberry Pi 5, is now crowdfunding.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Dutch home automation specialist DomeCtrl has launched a crowdfunding campaign for the ControlBox, a controller designed to integrate hydronic underfloor heating systems with Home Assistant. The device manages thermal valves, hydraulic PWM pumps, and 1-Wire temperature sensors over long cables ( 6 m), while offering 32 LED-strip output channels capable of delivering up to 2 kW.

The ControlBox is built around a Raspberry Pi 5 (4GB), paired with a 128GB NVMe SSD to run a full Home Assistant server. A previously announced Espressif ESP32-based variant (unveiled in November 2024) has been shelved for now. The controller features four 1.5A mains outputs, 16x 24VAC 500mA outputs for valves, three opto-isolated GPIO, two 24VDC PWM outputs, three opto-isolated inputs, and a 433MHz transceiver for wireless peripherals.

Key features include per-room software thermostats, automated pre-heating schedules, pump shutdown when no heat is requested, optimized solar heat collector control via PID regulation, and automatic floor heating shutdown when alternative heat sources (like wood stoves) are active. Hardware schematics will be released under an unspecified open-hardware license on GitHub after the official launch.

The crowdfunding campaign is live on Crowd Supply at $1,550, including the Raspberry Pi 5 and SSD. Shipments are expected by the end of July 2027, though crowdfunding fulfillment risks apply.
