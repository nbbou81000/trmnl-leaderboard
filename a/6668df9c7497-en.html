Magnetic PCB Holder: 3D-Printed Snap-On Work Surface for Soldering

A 3D-printed magnetic holder designed to secure PCBs during soldering or rework tasks.

A 3D-printed magnetic work holder, designed to snap onto any steel print surface or ferrous work surface, securely holds PCBs during soldering, probing, rework, and photography. Created by TYnextnext, this low-profile tool features adjustable clamps and offers a fresh alternative to traditional helping-hands or third-hand devices.

The holder requires M4×12mm bolts, M4 nuts, and 8×2mm or 10×2mm neodymium disc magnets for installation. Design files are available for download on MakerWorld. For heat resistance, PETG or ABS is recommended, though PLA can work on cooler surfaces. A 20-35% infill with 3-4 perimeters is advised. Magnets are press-fit, and CA glue can be used optionally for extra hold. Proper magnet polarity alignment is crucial to ensure a strong grip on the plate.
