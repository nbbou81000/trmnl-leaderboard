STM32H7P: ST Unveils High-Performance 520MHz MCU for Embedded AI

STMicroelectronics expands its STM32 lineup with the STM32H7P, a microcontroller optimized for embedded AI and real-time processing in industrial automation.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

STMicroelectronics has introduced the STM32H7P, a new microcontroller (MCU) from the STM32H7 family, designed for smart embedded systems. The manufacturer claims this component combines an Arm Cortex-M7 core running at 520 MHz with two dedicated hardware accelerators: a Neural-ART coprocessor capable of delivering up to 85 giga-operations per second (GOPS) for embedded AI (edge-AI) computations, and a DSP (Digital Signal Processor) optimized for signal processing tasks such as Fast Fourier Transform (FFT). These features deliver up to 1.7 times the performance of competing solutions, according to STMicro.

The STM32H7P targets industrial applications, automation, energy management (solar, digital power supplies), and motor control. It also includes advanced analog peripherals, such as analog-to-digital (ADC) and digital-to-analog (DAC) converters, as well as timers inherited from the STM32G5 series. Its design features a Tightly-Coupled Memory (TCM) architecture to reduce memory latency, and is rated for operation in temperatures up to 125 °C.

STMicro has announced immediate availability for sampling, with production scheduled for January 2027. The price is set at $4.43 per unit for the 1MB LQFP100 package variant (10,000-unit quantities). A dedicated Discovery Kit development board will launch in January. Technical details are available on the official product page.
