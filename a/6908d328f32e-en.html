CantaStorie: A Cloud-Free AI Storytelling Toy

A screen-free educational toy leverages local AI to craft personalized stories from recognized objects, without internet access.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

AI-powered toys that converse or tell stories raise privacy concerns due to their reliance on cloud services. To address this, SuperModerno created CantaStorie, a screen-free toy that runs its entire AI pipeline locally. Built around an Arduino UNO Q, it features a camera, speaker, and electronic components housed in a 3D-printed cyclops-like design.

The toy identifies objects placed in front of it using a lightweight YOLOX-Nano vision system, filtered to ensure detections exceed 75% confidence. A whitelist restricts recognized objects, preventing background elements from triggering unintended stories. Once identified, CantaStorie combines the object with parent-configured details (child’s name, hobbies) to generate a story using a local language model like Gemma 1B or Qwen 3.5 0.8B. The text is converted to speech via a local text-to-speech engine and played through an integrated speaker.

Physical interactions enhance engagement: two movable arms with potentiometers allow modifying parameters that influence story generation. An addressable LED ring surrounds the camera, serving as both illumination for the vision system and an activity indicator during processing. The final prototype reduces the delay between object detection and story playback to about 27 seconds, down from two and a half minutes in earlier versions, following software refactoring and model adjustments.
