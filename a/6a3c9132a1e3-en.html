A Self-Contained LoRa GPS Pet Collar Without Subscription

A Meshtastic-based GPS pet tracker using LoRa, with no subscription fees or dedicated network, featuring dual power modes and modular design.

Commercial pet trackers often require subscriptions or connections to proprietary networks. To bypass these limitations, Jon G Aguado developed LIA, a self-contained collar tracker leveraging the Meshtastic protocol and LoRa technology.

The device features a custom PCB hosting an ESP32-S3 microcontroller, a LoRa module for long-range communication, a GPS/GNSS receiver, and a charging circuit compatible with a 18650 or 5023450 Li-ion pouch cell. The components are enclosed in a 3D-printed case, sealed against weather with a magnetic 4-pin pogo USB connector for charging.

Two power modes are available: a high-power mode that updates the location every 30 seconds and activates bright LEDs for visual tracking, and a low-power mode that reduces updates to once per minute. A third option completely disables the GPS to maximize battery life. The designer notes that this full deactivation feature is rarely offered by commercial manufacturers.

The project warns about FCC compliance issues for US users deploying Meshtastic and LoRa. An alternative for pet tracking without this solution is mentioned as the KineFox project.
