New MSX Sound Expansion Cartridge with OPN3 Chip

A DIY kit adds an OPN3 chip to an MSX for rich FM synthesis sound, reminiscent of the 1980s-1990s era.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Owners of an MSX, the 8-bit Japanese computer from the 1980s-1990s, now have a way to enhance its sound capabilities. The Misato MSX kit, designed by Ricardo Suzuki, includes a Yamaha YMF288 (OPN3) chip capable of generating three SSG (Software Sound Generator) channels, six FM channels, and six rhythm channels. This component, iconic of the FM synthesis era, delivers rich sounds typical of music from that period.

The kit comes as a DIY cartridge. Once installed, it offers two audio output options: a dedicated stereo jack on the cartridge, or a mono output mixed back into the MSX’s internal mixer. It allows users to play existing OPN3 compositions or create their own tracks using compatible software tools. This project targets retro computing enthusiasts looking to upgrade their machines without resorting to expensive proprietary solutions.

Comments highlight the appeal of such projects for retro machine users, while questioning software compatibility with existing libraries. No details are provided about the kit’s availability or pricing.
