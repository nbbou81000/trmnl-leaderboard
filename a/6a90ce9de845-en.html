3D Creations and Electronics Projects for the Holidays

Adafruit shares three DIY projects combining 3D printing and electronics for the holidays.

In the latest 3D Hangouts session, Adafruit highlights three DIY projects blending 3D printing and electronics, ideal for the holiday season. The first is a Lego brick-inspired holiday wreath, designed to assemble like a puzzle. This project uses NeoPixels, addressable LED strips, for customizable lighting effects. A detailed guide is available on Adafruit’s website to recreate this decoration.

The second project is a DIY version of the Apple HomeView, a home dashboard. This build relies on a Metro ESP32-P4, an Arduino-compatible microcontroller board, and a 7-inch touchscreen to display real-time information. The project is modular and adaptable for other home automation needs.

Finally, Adafruit features an original 3D model: a twisty stress-relief eyeball, inspired by fidget toys. This model, shared via Creality Cloud, is designed for 3D printing and mechanical animation. A demonstration video shows its operation.

These projects showcase the synergy between 3D printing and electronics, with components like NeoPixels or ESP32-P4 boards. Adafruit encourages the community to share their own creations combining these two fields.
