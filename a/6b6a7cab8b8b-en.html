Tapmora Unveils Battery-Free E Ink Photo Magnet for $45

Tapmora’s E Ink Photo Magnet displays smartphone photos via NFC tap, with no wires or external power needed.

Tapmora, a brand known for its E Ink innovations, has launched the world’s first battery-free E Ink Photo Magnet, a wireless, pocket-sized digital frame that transfers smartphone photos to a paper-like display via NFC. The device requires no charging cables, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth and is priced at $45, available for purchase directly from the company’s website.

The Tapmora E Ink Photo Magnet features a Spectra 6 full-color array, a six-color E Ink panel that ensures accurate color reproduction—unlike cheaper four-color portable panels that often produce muddy grays in landscapes or skin tones. Its magnetic, matte, paper-like display blends seamlessly into refrigerators or desks, offering a premium, organic aesthetic compared to harsh, glowing LCD screens.

However, the device has notable trade-offs. Since the screen relies entirely on a brief burst of energy harvested from the smartphone’s NFC chip during photo transfers, image updates take a few seconds, and there is no backlighting or manual refresh buttons. Positioned as a static canvas rather than an interactive gadget, it appeals to minimalist enthusiasts looking to replace instant film or static prints but may disappoint those seeking responsive functionality.
