New Rockchip RK3576 SMARC 2.2 Module for Edge AI Applications

MYiR introduces a SMARC 2.2 module powered by Rockchip RK3576 SoC, targeting Edge AI with a 6 TOPS NPU.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

MYiR has launched the MYC-JR3576, a SMARC 2.2 system-on-module (SoM) powered by Rockchip’s RK3576 or industrial-grade RK3576J SoC. The octa-core processor combines four Cortex-A72 cores at 2.2GHz and four Cortex-A53 cores at 1.8GHz, alongside a Cortex-M0 core running at 400MHz. It features a 6 TOPS (INT8) neural processing unit (NPU) supporting INT4, INT8, INT16, BF16, and TF32 operations.

The SoM offers up to 8GB of LPDDR5 RAM and 64GB of eMMC storage, along with an ARM Mali-G52 MC3 GPU supporting OpenGL ES 1.1/2.0/3.2, OpenCL 2.0, and Vulkan 1.1. Video capabilities include hardware decoding for H.264, H.265, VP9, AV1, and AVS2 up to 8K@30fps or 4K@120fps, and encoding for H.264/H.265 up to 4K@60fps. All I/Os are exposed via a 314-pin MXM 3.0 connector compliant with SMARC 2.2.

A companion development board, the MYD-JR3576, provides additional interfaces including M.2 slots for NVMe SSDs, HDMI and DisplayPort outputs, dual MIPI-CSI camera interfaces, a microSD slot, dual Gigabit Ethernet ports, and an M.2 socket for 4G/5G LTE modules. The board also includes isolated RS232/RS485, CAN FD, and USB 2.0/3.0 ports.

MYiR offers software support with Linux 6.1 (BSP for Debian/Yocto) and Android 15, including open-source drivers for peripherals. The modules are available in commercial (0~70°C) and industrial (-40~85°C) grades, priced at $209 and $192 respectively for 8GB/64GB and 4GB/32GB configurations. The development kit is offered at $293 or $279 depending on the included module.
