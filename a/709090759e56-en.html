Valve Steam Frame: CAD Files and Docs for VR Tinkerers

Valve releases CAD files and hardware references for the Steam Frame VR headset, enabling custom mods and accessories.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Valve has released a hardware reference repository for its newly launched Steam Frame VR headset, providing everything needed for custom modifications. The repository includes 3D models in STEP format for the core module, head strap, and controllers, allowing users to design add-ons like carrying cases or mounts without physical measurements.

The files also specify the positions of the IR tracking LEDs embedded in the controllers, ensuring designs won’t block their visibility. A user-accessible expansion port on the front of the headset exposes two interfaces: a high-speed MIPI camera and a PCI Express bus. The documentation details this port and includes reference designs in KiCad and Altium for building compatible circuits.

This move aligns with Valve’s history of supporting tinkerer-friendly hardware. With demand for the Steam Frame far outstripping supply, early adopters on the waitlist can use the repository to start planning their customizations before receiving their device.
