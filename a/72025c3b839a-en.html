ESP-Mosaico: A Modular ESP32-S31 Platform for AI and Hardware Prototyping

Espressif launches a modular ESP32-S31 platform with touchscreen, built-in AI tools, and expansion modules for rapid prototyping.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Espressif has unveiled ESP-Mosaico, a development platform built around the ESP32-S31, aimed at simplifying AI integration into hardware projects. The board features a 480 × 480 OLED touchscreen, haptic feedback, an onboard battery, and USB Type-C connectivity. Its architecture combines a dual-core RISC-V setup (a high-performance HP core at 320 MHz and a low-power LP core) with 16 MB of PSRAM and 128 MB of flash storage.

The platform is tailored for applications such as modular magnetic toys, square touchscreen HMIs, and edge voice/multimedia systems. Two expansion modules are already available: a camera module (3.2 MP or 1 MP) and a multi-function interaction module featuring a light sensor, PIR motion detector, IR LED, and addressable RGB LEDs.

Espressif highlights two software tools to streamline development: ESP-Claw, which generates code from natural language instructions, and ESP-ZeroCode AI, which automates design, deployment, and testing on physical hardware. Documentation is split between Espressif’s technical site and a dedicated ESP-Mosaico guide covering these development modes.

The full devkit, including the baseboard, camera module, and interaction module, is now available for $59 on the ESP-Mosaico store. An optional $15 battery module is also offered.
