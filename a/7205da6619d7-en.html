Nuvoton Unveils Ultra-Compact Evaluation Board for Industrial AI

Nuvoton introduces Chili Pro, an ultra-compact Linux evaluation board featuring a heterogeneous MA35D0K processor with dual Cortex-A35 cores and a real-time Cortex-M4.

Nuvoton expands its NuMicro MA35D0 series with three new LQFP128 microprocessors (MPUs): MA35D05KH67C, MA35D05KI67C, and MA35D05KJ67C. Designed for industrial AI, smart gateways, and renewable energy systems, these heterogeneous chips feature dual 64-bit Arm Cortex-A35 cores running up to 650 MHz, alongside a dedicated Arm Cortex-M4 core at 180 MHz for real-time tasks.

The accompanying evaluation board, NuMaker-IoT-MA35D0-A2 (Chili Pro), stands out for its compact size despite full-sized ports. Measuring 45 × 45 mm, it includes standard RJ45 and USB connectors along with other interfaces. While larger than some reference boards like the VoCore2 (25.4 × 25.4 mm) or Milk-V Duo 256M (51 × 21 mm), it remains one of the smallest Linux-capable platforms available, per the manufacturer. Nuvoton even claims it is the "industry’s smallest Linux platform."
