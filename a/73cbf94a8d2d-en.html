Building a Desk Lamp with Flexible LED Filaments

A maker designs a desk lamp using flexible LED filaments, which are durable and easy to power, though mechanical challenges remain.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Eric Weinhoffer has crafted a desk lamp using flexible LED filaments, soft light strands that outperform EL wire in brightness and ease of powering, though they remain somewhat fragile—several broke during repeated assembly and disassembly. The electrical setup relies on a 3.7 V LiPo battery paired with a PowerBoost module, which boosts the voltage to 5 V for USB charging. A 3.3 V regulator then steps it down for the LEDs, while resistors limit the current flowing through each filament.

The trickiest part was securing the filaments in place. All parts were 3D-printed on a Form 4 SLA machine, borrowing from machining techniques. The design features a collet plate and matching nut plate at each end, with tapered surfaces that clamp three collets around the filaments as an M4 bolt is tightened. Build notes suggest future improvements, such as a custom PCB and a flatter retention system like a flexure plate.
