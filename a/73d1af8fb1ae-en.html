Wio Tracker L2 Pro: Standalone Meshtastic Terminal with Touchscreen and GPS

Seeed Studio unveils a smartphone-free Meshtastic device featuring a 3.2" touchscreen and integrated LoRa module.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Seeed Studio has released the Wio Tracker L2 Pro, a standalone Meshtastic device that eliminates the need for a smartphone, tablet, or computer. The device features a 3.2-inch color touchscreen (320×240 resolution) for direct interaction, a feature absent in previous Wio Tracker models.

The core of the device is the Wio-S3 module, which integrates an ESP32-S3R8 microcontroller (dual-core up to 240 MHz) and a Semtech SX1262 LoRa transceiver. It supports both European (868 MHz) and US (915 MHz) LoRa bands, as well as 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). The Wio Tracker L2 Pro also includes a GNSS receiver for positioning, a microSD slot for offline maps, and physical controls like programmable buttons, LEDs, a microphone, and a small speaker.

Power is provided by a 3,000 mAh battery, rechargeable via USB Type-C or an optional solar panel. The firmware, based on the Meshtastic project, offers two user interfaces: a basic version and a more polished MUI. LoRa connectivity, enhanced by Meshtastic’s mesh networking, delivers a claimed maximum range of 5.8 km in line-of-sight conditions. The Wio Tracker L2 Pro is available for $59.42 (a 15% discount off the planned retail price) from the Seeed Studio store, with shipments expected within 4–6 weeks.
