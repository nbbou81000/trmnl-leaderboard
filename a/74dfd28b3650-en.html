A USB-C Charger Turned into a Pocket-Sized Adjustable Power Supply

A 2 mm-thick PCB turns any USB-C charger into a 48 V, 5 A adjustable power supply.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

The Negotiator II leverages USB Power Delivery (USB PD) 3.1 to turn any standard USB-C charger into an adjustable power supply. Housed in a business-card-sized PCB (2 mm thick), it features an LED bar showing available voltages from 5 V up to 48 V in 0.1 V increments. Buttons let users select the desired voltage, while dedicated displays provide live voltage, current, and power readings. The device can deliver up to 5 A depending on the charger, cable, and PD mode in use.

The Wire Wedge system allows quick connections without connectors: a stripped wire slides into a PCB slot and is pulled parallel to the board, securing it in place. A second USB port connects the device to a web-based console for real-time graphs, diagnostics, and firmware updates. The cost of the four-layer ENIG boards is estimated at $8 each for orders of 100 units. The design will be open-sourced in the future.
