Extrudio: Simulating 3D Prints from G-Code

A tool converts G-Code into realistic 3D print renders, including manufacturing defects.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

CAD software often includes basic rendering tools to visualize parts, but these renders show the model’s geometry rather than the actual 3D printed result. Extrudio addresses this gap by generating realistic images directly from G-Code, the instruction set your 3D printer follows to build an object.

Unlike traditional methods that start from a 3D model (STL file), Extrudio simulates the layer-by-layer filament deposition process, faithfully replicating common printing defects. Layer lines, stair-stepping on curves, and overhang artifacts are accurately displayed, providing a preview close to the real printed outcome. The tool also lets users place the part in various virtual environments (bench, kitchen, neutral background) for tailored presentations.

According to its website, Extrudio targets sellers of 3D printed parts, enabling them to quickly generate realistic images without physically printing every prototype. It also simplifies evaluating different parameters (colors, printing techniques) before production. While it doesn’t replace actual photographs, the tool offers a fast alternative for projects where time or resources are limited.
