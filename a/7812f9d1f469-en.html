ControlBox: A Home Automation Controller for Underfloor Heating and Lighting (Crowdfunding)

A Raspberry Pi 5-based home automation controller running Home Assistant, designed for underfloor heating, lighting, and 1-Wire sensors, is now crowdfunding.

DomeCtrl’s ControlBox is a home automation controller built around a Raspberry Pi 5 with 4GB RAM and a 128GB NVMe SSD preloaded with Home Assistant. Designed primarily for hydronic radiant floor heating (RFH) systems, it also handles lighting control and 1-Wire temperature sensors. The system manages thermal valves, PWM hydraulic pumps, and 24V AC devices like floor heating valves, alongside up to 32 PWM-controlled LED strip channels (2 kW total).

The device features multiple communication interfaces: a half-duplex RS485 bus, Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5 via the Raspberry Pi, and a W32-ESP01 header for ESPHome control. It also supports 433 MHz modules for wireless peripherals such as curtain rails, garage doors, or smart switches. Two 1-Wire channels allow connecting up to 30 temperature sensors over cables longer than 6 meters.

Input/output options include 3 opto-isolated GPIO outputs, 2 opto-isolated PWM outputs (24V DC max), 3 opto-isolated GPIO inputs (5-24V DC), and 4 power input connectors (24V DC, up to 20A per pin). The 32 PWM outputs for LED strips support up to 10A per connector with configurable frequency (default: 200Hz). Power input ranges from 100-240V AC, with 4 solid-state relay outputs (1.5A) and 16 24V AC relay outputs (500mA) included. The enclosure has an IP30 rating.

Launched on Crowd Supply, the fully assembled ControlBox is priced at $1,550 with free shipping in the US and $18 internationally. Hardware documentation and schematics are available on GitHub, while Home Assistant integrations for LEDs and 24V AC outputs are hosted on dedicated repositories. The manufacturer notes that lowering room temperatures by 3°C for 12 hours daily could reduce winter energy consumption by up to 7%.
