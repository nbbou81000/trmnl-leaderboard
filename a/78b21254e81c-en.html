Restoring a Fluke Model 407 High-Voltage Power Supply

A vintage high-voltage power supply is rescued by identifying a faulty capacitor through careful troubleshooting.

A vintage Fluke Model 407 high-voltage power supply, featuring vacuum tubes and circuit boards, was saved from the scrap heap after careful troubleshooting. Though it powered on, the device failed to deliver the correct output voltage. To avoid potential damage, the repairer used an isolated transformer paired with a current-limited variac for a gradual and safe power-up, a method that could also be replicated with a dim-bulb tester built from spare parts.

Visual inspection revealed no obvious damage, so the focus shifted to signal and component testing, aided by the original schematics. The fault was traced to a faulty capacitor in a feedback loop responsible for regulating the output voltage. While not a full restoration, the process highlights a methodical approach to diagnosing hardware issues in older electronics, considering their specific challenges.
