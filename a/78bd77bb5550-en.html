The TP223 Capacitive Touch Sensor: A Mechanical Switch Alternative

An affordable capacitive touch module to replace mechanical switches, working through various materials.

Mechanical switches, while durable, can wear out or get clogged over time. The TP223 capacitive touch sensor provides a modern and cost-effective alternative for adding touch controls to electronic projects. This inexpensive module detects touch through materials like plastic, glass, or wood, allowing for the design of control panels without moving parts.

The sensor’s sensitivity depends on the thickness of the material placed above it: thicker surfaces reduce responsiveness. To adjust sensitivity, you can shrink the touch area, add an external capacitor, or use a thicker panel. The TP223 consumes less than 2 microamps in standby mode, making it suitable for battery-powered projects. By default, it keeps a high signal as long as the touch is detected, but it can be configured to toggle between two states with each press by soldering specific jumpers. An additional capacitor can also be added to reduce false triggers.

This module serves as a simple solution for replacing mechanical switches in DIY electronics projects, especially in 3D-printed designs. It could even function as a power button with minor modifications. Its low cost and ease of integration make it an appealing choice for makers looking to modernize their creations.
