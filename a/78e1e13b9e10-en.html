BIOSTAR EdgeComp MS-U320: Fanless Industrial Mini PC with COM Ports and 2.5GbE

A fanless industrial mini PC powered by Intel Wildcat Lake SoCs, featuring four COM ports and 4G/5G connectivity.

The BIOSTAR EdgeComp MS-U320 is a fanless industrial mini PC built around Intel’s Wildcat Lake SoCs. It is available with either a Core 3 304 or Core 5 320 processor, offering between 5 and 6 cores with turbo frequencies up to 4.6 GHz. The integrated Intel Xe3 GPU, available in one or two cores, delivers up to 20 TOPS, while a dedicated NPU provides 15 or 16 TOPS for hardware-accelerated tasks.

The system supports up to 64GB of DDR5 memory and NVMe storage via a PCIe Gen4 x2 M.2 2242/2280 slot. For display output, it includes an HDMI 2.1 port (4K@60Hz) and a DisplayPort 1.4 (4K@60Hz), enabling dual independent displays. Networking is handled by two Intel I226 2.5GbE controllers, with optional WiFi/Bluetooth or 4G/5G connectivity via dedicated M.2 slots.

A key feature is its four serial ports (RS232/RS422/RS485) via DB9 connectors, ideal for industrial applications. Additional connectivity includes six USB ports (two USB 3.2 Gen2 Type-A and four USB 2.0), 3.5mm audio jacks, and a 9-24V DC power input. The compact form factor (167 x 135 x 60 mm) and 1.2 kg weight make it easy to deploy. It operates in temperatures from -20°C to +50°C and supports 5% to 90% relative humidity.

Compatible with Windows 11, Ubuntu 24.04, and Windows 11 IoT Enterprise 24H2, the EdgeComp MS-U320 targets industrial automation, IoT, and smart retail. Pricing and availability details have not yet been announced.
