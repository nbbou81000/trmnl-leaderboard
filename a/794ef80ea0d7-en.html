Adafruit Unveils 8-Channel Clock Generator Board Up to 200 MHz

A precise and versatile clock generator for RF and electronics projects, now in stock.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

No more hunting for the right crystal with Adafruit’s Si5351C, an 8-channel TCXO clock generator. Built around a 25 MHz temperature-compensated reference, it produces frequencies from 2.5 kHz to 200 MHz on six channels (CLK0–CLK5) and 20 kHz to 50 MHz on the remaining two (CLK6–CLK7).

The Si5351C uses I2C to control internal phase-locked loops (PLL) and frequency dividers, enabling precise and arbitrary signal generation. Each output is independent and can be configured separately. By default, signals output 3 V peak-to-peak, but amplitude can be lowered via dedicated pads (VDDA/B/C/D).

For RF applications, four outputs are accessible via edge-launch SMA connectors (sold separately). The remaining pins use a STEMMA QT connector, compatible with SparkFun’s Qwiic ecosystem, allowing solderless connections. A built-in 3.3 V LDO and level shifters support 3 V or 5 V logic and power.

A tutorial and software libraries (Arduino, CircuitPython, Python) are provided to simplify integration. The board is available now and ships immediately.
