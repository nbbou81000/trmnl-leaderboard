3D-Printed Enclosure for HC-SR501 PIR Sensor and PC817 Optocoupler

A 3D-printed enclosure designed for a motion-trigger module used in Halloween animatronics.

A new 3D-printed enclosure is available for assembling a DIY motion-trigger module, tailored for Halloween animatronics. The design includes a HC-SR501 PIR motion sensor and a Sharp PC817 optocoupler, enabling motion-triggered actions.

The required components are a 9V battery clip (Type T), a square 2-pin rocker switch (KCD1), a 100Ω resistor, and a 9V battery. The enclosure can be printed in PLA or PETG, with an adjustable TPU cap. The provided files use standard print settings: 0.2mm layer height, 6 walls, 25% infill, no supports, with an estimated print time of 1.4 hours per part.

Assembly and wiring are straightforward, with a wiring diagram included in an attached photo (to be verified before connection). This project targets makers and electronics enthusiasts looking to integrate motion detection into robotic or animatronic creations.
