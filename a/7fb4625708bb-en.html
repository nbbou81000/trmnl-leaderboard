Arduino Launches USB Camera for Embedded AI and Computer Vision

Arduino unveils a USB camera designed to simplify embedded AI and computer vision on its UNO Q and VENTUNO Q boards.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Arduino has launched its first standalone USB camera, designed to streamline embedded AI and real-time computer vision integration in electronic projects. Unlike traditional solutions requiring specific drivers or kernel compilations, this camera plugs directly into the USB ports of the UNO Q and VENTUNO Q boards with minimal setup.

The device combines HD video capture with an integrated microphone, enabling simultaneous image and audio processing for multimodal applications. A touch-dimmable LED ring with adjustable color temperature ensures consistent lighting, while versatile mounting options (clip, flat stand, or tripod thread) simplify installation.

The key innovation lies in its compatibility with AI models available in Arduino App Lab, allowing direct execution of algorithms like YOLO-X or MobileNet on the host boards. Potential use cases include object detection, touchless gesture control via MediaPipe, or generating textual scene descriptions using vision-language models like Qwen VLM. The system also supports building interactive assistants that combine voice and visual recognition.

A recent demonstration by Andrea Richetta (Arduino) showcased a real-time facial recognition system built from scratch, leveraging the new camera’s capabilities. Technical documentation is available to explore these features.
