New $21 ESP32-P4X board with Ethernet and WiFi 6

A $21 ESP32-P4X development board integrates Ethernet, WiFi 6, RS485, CAN Bus, and MIPI interfaces for displays and cameras.

Wireless Tag’s WT99P461-S1 development board is built around the WT01P461-S1 module, combining an ESP32-P4X (v3.2) and an ESP32-C61. The latter handles WiFi 6 and Bluetooth 5.4, while the former features a dual-core RISC-V processor running at 360/400 MHz with AI acceleration and single-precision FPU, making it suitable for industrial applications.

The board includes extensive connectivity options: a 10/100 Mbps Ethernet port, RS485 and CAN Bus interfaces, and a microSD card slot. It also integrates an onboard microphone, a speaker connector (up to 3W), and MIPI DSI (display) and MIPI CSI (camera) connectors. Additional features include an RGB LED and reset/boot buttons.

The module ships with a firmware based on the ESP-Brookesia framework, featuring a smartphone-like UI, AI vision demos, MP3 audio playback, and MJPEG video support (H.264 not yet supported). It also supports 7-inch touchscreens (1024×600 resolution). Users can flash custom firmware via a USB Type-C port or a dedicated programming interface.

Two purchase options are available: the bare board for $21 or a kit including a 7-inch display for $42.50. Orders are handled directly through Wireless Tag’s website, with shipping and taxes added at checkout.
