An e-ink screen that draws the birds outside your window

An AI-powered e-ink screen displays real-time drawings of birds detected by a microphone, using vintage illustrations.

An innovative project uses artificial intelligence to turn a simple natural observation into a dynamic artwork. A large e-ink screen, installed in Norway, displays real-time drawings of birds seen or heard outside. Behind this setup is a Raspberry Pi 5, paired with a USB microphone and specialized sound analysis software: BirdNet-Go.

This AI-powered tool identifies bird species from their songs. The displayed images come from 19th-century bird-watching guides, creating an evolving collage. The screen doesn’t just show random birds—it reflects a representative selection of local European species, offering an accurate snapshot of the surrounding biodiversity.

The project’s creator highlights the creative aspect of this approach, avoiding AI-generated images in favor of historical illustrations. A feature even allows users to check the list of currently detected species in real time, confirming the interactive nature of the setup.

While similar projects exist, this one stands out for its retro aesthetic and use of real data rather than artificially generated content. Comments also praise its originality, though some users mention comparable projects already in existence.
