Sipeed Launches SLogic32U3, a 1.4 GS/s Logic Analyzer for $149

Sipeed has opened crowdfunding for the SLogic32U3, a high-end logic analyzer boasting up to 1.4 GS/s sampling rates.

Sipeed, a Chinese embedded and hobbyist electronics specialist, has launched a crowdfunding campaign for the third-generation SLogic32U3 logic analyzer. The device delivers professional-grade performance at an affordable price, thanks to a custom 10 Gbps FPGA IP and USB 3.2 Gen. 2 connectivity. It supports up to 32 channels, with sampling rates scaling from 1.4 GS/s on four channels down to 200 MS/s on 32 channels.

The SLogic32U3 raises the maximum input frequency to 350 MHz, compared to 200 MHz on its predecessor, the SLogic16U3. It maintains a 0–10V input voltage range and an adjustable 0–6V logic trigger threshold. Data streaming to the host PC reaches 800 MB/s, double the previous generation, with a 256 MB DDR3 streaming buffer.

A new feature in this model is an optional analog-to-digital conversion (ADC) module, adding four 8-bit channels at 100 MS/s, sold in pairs for $19. The analyzer is compatible with open-source software like ngscopeclient and includes a browser-based, installation-free web app called SLogicWeb. This app works on Linux, Windows, macOS, Android, and any WebUSB-capable browser, offering over 150 protocol decoders from the Sigrok project.

The Kickstarter campaign offers the SLogic32U3 starting at $149, with shipping expected in November. The optional ADC modules are available for $19 per pair.
