3D-Printed Enclosure for Arduino UNO R4: Gyroid Infill as Design Feature

A 3D-printed enclosure for Arduino UNO R4 featuring visible gyroid infill and tool-free mounting.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

A user shares on Maker World a protective enclosure designed specifically for the Arduino UNO R4 board. The case is printed without top or bottom solid layers, revealing a 15% gyroid infill pattern as both a design and functional feature. Four strategically placed nubs grip the Arduino’s mounting holes, securing the board without screws or additional fasteners.

The design is expandable to accommodate shield housings without altering the base structure. The example shown uses Eryone Dual-Silk PLA, printed at 0.2mm layer height, with three walls and 15% infill. The final weight is around 11 grams, with an estimated print time of 37 minutes.
