Sipeed SLogic32U3: 10 Gbps 32-Channel Logic Analyzer Available via Crowdfunding

Sipeed unveils the world’s first 10 Gbps USB 3.2 logic analyzer, the SLogic32U3, featuring up to 32 channels and 350 MHz bandwidth via crowdfunding.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

The Sipeed SLogic32U3 is marketed as the world’s first USB 3.2 Gen 2x1 logic analyzer capable of 10 Gbps, succeeding the 3.2 Gbps SLogic16U3 released in 2025. It supports up to 32 channels distributed across four mini HDMI connectors (8 channels per port) and offers a digital signal bandwidth of 350 MHz.

Sampling rates depend on the number of active channels: up to 1,400 MS/s (mega-samples per second) for 4 channels, 800 MS/s for 8 channels, 400 MS/s for 16 channels, and 200 MS/s when using all 32 channels. A 2 Gbit DDR3 FIFO buffer temporarily stores data before transfer to a PC. Real-time capture mode is constrained by the computer’s memory or storage capacity.

The input accepts signal voltages from 0 to 10 V, with an adjustable threshold to match different logic levels. An optional ADC module is available for expanded functionality. The device is currently available via crowdfunding, with no specified release date or pricing.
