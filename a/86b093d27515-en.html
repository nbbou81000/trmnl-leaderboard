Articulated Rexagon: A Fully Articulated T-Rex/Dragon Hybrid 3D Printable Without Supports

A fully articulated 3D-printable hybrid combining T-Rex and dragon features, printable without supports in 2 or 3 colors.

The Articulated Rexagon model by PinkyWings merges T-Rex and dragon traits into a single fully articulated piece. Its smooth joints allow varied poses without requiring print supports, streamlining the printing process.

Available in 2-color and 3-color versions, the model suits different 3D printer setups. Print times range from 1.6 to 5.9 hours depending on the variant. For Bambu H2D printers, a 2-color optimized version cuts print time to 1.6 hours. Recommended settings include a 0.2 mm layer height, 2 walls, and 5 % infill.

Originally designed for Halloween, this model has been reintegrated into the regular lineup of articulated flexibles. Commercial use requires a Patreon membership. The file is shared as part of Adafruit’s weekly #3DThursday celebrations, highlighting DIY 3D printing projects from the community.
