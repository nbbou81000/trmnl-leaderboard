3D-Printed Mobile Case for Raspberry Pi Zero with USB-C Charging and Battery

A compact 3D-printed case for Raspberry Pi Zero W includes a 2000mAh LiPo battery and USB-C charging, perfect for mobile and low-power projects.

This 3D-printed enclosure is designed for the Raspberry Pi Zero W, turning it into a portable, self-contained system. The compact case houses a 2000mAh LiPo battery and a 5V boost converter module, ensuring stable power for mobile or low-power applications.

Key features include a USB Type-C charging port, a dedicated microSD card access slot, and a physical MN1 power switch for safe shutdown without risking data corruption. The tight-fitting design minimizes cable clutter while protecting internal components.

This project targets users seeking a discreet, energy-efficient solution, such as e-ink displays, embedded dashboards, or mobile home automation setups. The STL files for 3D printing are available for free download.
