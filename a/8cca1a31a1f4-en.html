Q8botOne: A Hand-Sized Open-Source Quadruped Robot

A compact, open-source quadruped robot powered by ESP32-C3 and DYNAMIXEL actuators.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

ZeroWire Robotics’ Q8botOne is a palm-sized open-source quadruped robot. Its wire-free design integrates all components onto a central PCB that doubles as the structural chassis. Powered by DYNAMIXEL smart actuators and an ESP32-C3 microcontroller, the robot can jog, jump, and carry up to twice its 250-gram weight.

The main board features a dedicated 6V/16A step-down converter for motor power and a battery management system for two 14500 Li-ion cells. A separate ESP32-C3-based control module is included, paired with an Adafruit Mini I2C Gamepad. The ESP32-C3 SoC, featuring a 32-bit RISC-V single-core processor up to 160 MHz, includes 400 KB SRAM and 4 MB embedded flash, optimized for low-power wireless applications.
