ATX Conversion Kit Revives Apple’s 2019 "Cheesegrater" Mac Pro

An engineer offers a kit to replace the proprietary internals of the 2019 Mac Pro 7,1 with standard ATX hardware, preserving its iconic design.

Mechanical engineer Stefan Bond has created a conversion kit that lets users install modern PC components inside the chassis of Apple’s 2019 Mac Pro 7,1, nicknamed the "Cheesegrater" for its distinctive ventilation grilles. Originally released in 2019, this model featured Intel Xeon W processors, up to 1.5TB of RAM, and Radeon Pro graphics cards, but its proprietary design restricted upgrades.

The kit, which is fully reversible, swaps out the original motherboard for a standard ATX motherboard, compatible with ATX power supplies and graphics cards. It accommodates ATX or smaller motherboards, graphics cards up to 308mm long and 140mm tall, and air coolers or all-in-one liquid cooling systems (up to 240mm with an optional accessory). Two drive bays for 2.5" or 3.5" SATA drives are included, while the original front fan, power button, and LED remain functional.

Bond explains that the kit is designed to fit the Mac Pro’s unique chassis geometry rather than forcing a generic PC case adaptation. Installation requires complete disassembly of the original internals and assembly of the provided components. Targeted at experienced PC builders, the kit is available for pre-order on Kickstarter starting at CA$699 (around $491 USD), with expected shipping in March 2027. As with all crowdfunding projects, delivery timelines and availability are not guaranteed.
