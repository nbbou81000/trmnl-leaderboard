Claude Now Generates Live Interactive Dashboards and Explainer Videos from Your Data

Anthropic introduces two AI tools to turn your data into live dashboards and editable explainer videos on demand.

Anthropic has expanded its AI toolkit with Claude Dashboard and Claude Motion, two features designed to automate real-time data analysis and presentation.

Claude Dashboard connects to various data sources to generate a clean, auto-updating dashboard. Key metrics are displayed live, providing an effortless way to monitor essential information without manual updates. The tool aims to make data accessible even for non-technical users.

Claude Motion takes dashboard insights further by creating short, editable explainer videos. Users can ask the AI to highlight specific trends or adjust the presentation. If the output isn’t satisfactory, a simple prompt can refine the video. This feature is currently in Team and Enterprise beta.

Anthropic has not clarified who can access Claude Dashboard in beta—whether it’s open to individuals or restricted to enterprises. Both tools leverage Anthropic’s language models to deliver dynamic, interactive data visualization.
