A Bartop Arcade Cabinet Turned into an 8-Bit Retro Terminal

A bartop arcade cabinet is repurposed as an 8-bit retro terminal featuring mechanical keyboard and terminal emulator for an authentic 80s feel.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

A bartop arcade cabinet has been repurposed as an 8-bit retro terminal inspired by the 1980s. Originally built as a standard arcade machine with a removable control panel (joystick and buttons), the device was temporarily modified to fit a mechanical keyboard in the empty space. This setup inspired the creation of a second dedicated panel featuring a clicky mechanical keyboard, a touchpad, and a small USB breakout.

The final design offers two modes: classic arcade with its dedicated controls, or a retro terminal mode where the display runs an 8-bit terminal emulator. The creator chose cool-retro-term, a terminal emulator designed to mimic the look of 1980s terminals, paired with a custom wallpaper featuring two 5.25" floppy drives. The wallpaper file is available for download on the project page.
