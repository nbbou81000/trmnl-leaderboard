ASRock Industrial Unveils NUC Core 300 Mini PCs and Motherboards with Intel Core 3/5 Processors

ASRock Industrial expands its NUC lineup with mini PCs and motherboards powered by Intel Core 3 304 and Core 5 320 processors from the Wildcat Lake-U series.

ASRock Industrial has introduced the NUC Core 300 BOX series, featuring mini PCs and motherboards built around Intel’s Core Series 3 Wildcat Lake-U processors. The lineup includes the NUC BOX-320, powered by an Intel Core 5 320 SoC, and the NUC BOX-304, based on a Core 3 304. The company also offers the NUC-320 and NUC-304 as standalone motherboards for custom builds.

The systems support up to 64GB of DDR5-6400 with in-band ECC, dual M.2 PCIe Gen4 storage slots, dual 2.5GbE Ethernet, Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3, triple-display output, USB 3.2 ports, and a 12–24V DC power input. These features, according to the manufacturer, make them suitable for industrial PCs, edge computing, and embedded applications.

The Core 3 304 (found in the NUC BOX-304 and NUC-304) features a 5-core CPU: one performance core (P-core) clocked up to 4.3 GHz and four low-power efficiency cores (LPE-cores) up to 3.3 GHz. It is paired with an Intel Xe3 Graphics integrated GPU.
