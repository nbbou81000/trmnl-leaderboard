CompuServe Web: A Functional Replica of the 1980s Online Service

A working recreation of the 1980s CompuServe online service is now accessible via web browser or Telnet terminal.

A developer has recreated CompuServe Web, a fully functional replica of the iconic 1980s online service. Built in Pascal, the project is accessible through a web browser or Telnet terminal, offering an authentic experience. Guest access provides a glimpse of the original interface and commands like GO CB or TOP, derived from a scanned user manual.

The project began as a simple online sales tool for the builder’s wife before evolving into a complete recreation. After years of spare-time development, the interface now includes key features: forums, news, weather updates, EasyPlex mail, file areas, and era-typical strategy games. Some sections remain empty due to the unavailability of original external databases (flight schedules, stock quotes, encyclopedia).

New account approval is manual and may take up to 24 hours. Unlike the original, user data and messages are encrypted. The creator, who first used CompuServe at 13 on a Commodore VIC-20 with a Z80 Video Pak running CP/M, relied on archives to restore manuals, including a SYSOP guide for system operators.
