Upgrading an iPhone Camera Lens to C-mount or EF-mount

A hacker replaces an iPhone’s stock lens with a C-mount or EF-mount to boost camera performance.

Modern smartphones feature high-quality image sensors, but their built-in lenses often lag behind despite manufacturers’ efforts. Hacker Evan Monsma previously replaced an iPhone’s stock lens with a C-mount unit, and now he’s back with an upgraded version.

The earlier mount, secured with cyanoacrylate glue, had detached. For this iteration, he machined a solid brass mount covering two of the iPhone’s three cameras, leaving the third untouched. The assembly was bonded with epoxy and protected by 3D-printed lens covers. A tripod thread was even added to the base of the mount.

The creator now tests the setup with both C-mount and EF-mount lenses, the latter delivering an extremely long effective focal length. He recommends adding an IR filter to prevent an unwanted pink tint in images. The original project, where he replaced a broken lens, is also documented.
