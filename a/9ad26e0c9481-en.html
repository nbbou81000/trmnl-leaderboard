M5Stack ToughC5: A Rugged ESP32-C5 Board for Outdoor IoT Projects

An ESP32-C5 in a weatherproof enclosure with touchscreen, Wi-Fi 6, and Zigbee/Thread support, designed for outdoor IoT projects.

M5Stack has unveiled the ToughC5, a development board built to withstand harsh outdoor conditions. Unlike standard boards, it features a weatherproof, UV-resistant enclosure that protects its components from rain, dust, and temperature fluctuations. At its core is an ESP32-C5 microcontroller, powered by a single-core RISC-V processor running up to 240 MHz, paired with 16 MB of flash and 8 MB of PSRAM.

Connectivity and features

The ToughC5 offers extensive connectivity options: dual-band Wi-Fi 6 (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz), Bluetooth 5 LE, and support for Zigbee and Thread via IEEE 802.15.4. These features enable connections to sensors, smart home devices, or industrial systems. A 2-inch IPS touchscreen (320×240 pixels) with a maximum brightness of 853 nits ensures readability even in direct sunlight. The enclosure also includes a passive buzzer for audio alerts, a microSD card slot for data storage, and an RX8130CE real-time clock powered by a CR2032 coin cell.

Four HY2.2-4P connectors expose interfaces such as RS485, I2C, UART, and GPIO, while two M12 waterproof cable entries allow external connections without compromising the enclosure’s integrity. Power options include USB-C, a 6-24 V input tied to the RS485 interface, or an external 3.7 V battery. Two power management chips (M5PM1 and M5IOE1) optimize power consumption, with a shutdown mode as low as 10.41 µA.

Development and availability

Software development is supported through the Arduino IDE, M5Stack’s M5Unified and M5GFX libraries, and an upcoming integration with the UiFlow2 graphical environment. Priced at $49.90, the ToughC5 is more expensive than a bare ESP32 board, but its integrated features—display, connectivity, power management, and protection—make it a compelling choice for outdoor IoT projects, environmental monitoring, or industrial sensor networks.
