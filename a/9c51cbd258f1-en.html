Waveshare Unveils ESP32-C5 Development Board with 3.5-inch Touch LCD

An ESP32-C5 development board featuring a 3.5-inch color touchscreen, optional camera, and built-in battery.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Waveshare has launched a new development board powered by the ESP32-C5 microcontroller from Espressif Systems. The ESP32-C5-Touch-LCD-3.5 stands out with its 3.5-inch color touchscreen, larger than typical solutions. The board also includes a speaker connector, an integrated microphone for voice interactions, and optional features.

Sensors include a 6-axis IMU for motion tracking, a temperature and humidity sensor, and a microSD card slot for additional storage. A 500 mAh battery is available as an option, along with a BF3901 low-resolution (0.08 MP) camera. A 32-pin GPIO connector enables further expansion.

The board is built around the ESP32-C5-WROOM-1U-N32R8 system-on-chip (SoC), featuring a single-core 32-bit RISC-V processor running up to 240 MHz and a low-power core at 40 MHz. It includes 384 KB of on-chip SRAM, 8 MB of PSRAM, and 32 MB of flash storage. Connectivity options include dual-band Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) with fallback to Wi-Fi 4 (802.11b/g/n), Bluetooth 5.0 LE, and an 802.15.4 radio for Zigbee 3.0 and Thread 1.3 protocols.
