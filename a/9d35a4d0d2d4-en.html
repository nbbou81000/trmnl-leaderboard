Jailbreak PS5: Running PS2 and Xbox Games via Emulation

Two emulation projects now let you run PlayStation 2 and Xbox games on a jailbroken PS5, though performance and compatibility differ.

The PlayStation 5, despite its locked-down design, can be turned into a versatile machine with a jailbreak. Two recent emulation projects now let you run original PlayStation 2 and Xbox games on the console.

The PS5X2 project, based on the PCSX2 emulator, delivers a polished port with GPU acceleration via Vulkan (thanks to the PS5Vulkan project). Many games run at full speed, even at resolutions higher than the original. In contrast, the XPSemu project—derived from the xemu Xbox emulator—is still in Alpha 1 stage, with limited compatibility, though Halo 2 already runs well.

Neither emulator supports playing games directly from original discs; a disc image must be stored on the PS5’s SSD. Additionally, a Wine port for the PS5 called PS5 Proton is in development to expand compatibility to Windows games, but no public release is available yet.
