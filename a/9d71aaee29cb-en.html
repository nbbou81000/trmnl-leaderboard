Reading 8 Analog Inputs on 2 ADC Pins with Arduino

An NXP HEF4052BP analog multiplexer lets you read 8 analog signals using just 2 Arduino ADC pins.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Robert Fletcher’s project on Hackster.io demonstrates how to read eight analog inputs using just two Arduino ADC pins. The key component is the NXP HEF4052BP, a dual 4-channel analog multiplexer. Each section of the chip routes one of four analog signals to a common output, with both sections sharing the same two address lines and an enable input.

In this setup, the first section connects to Arduino pin A0 and the second to A1. Eight 10 kΩ potentiometers simulate analog sensors, each wired between 5 V and ground with the wiper feeding a multiplexer input. The readings are printed to the Arduino Serial Monitor at 9600 baud.

The firmware selects a channel, waits for the signal to settle, discards the first ADC conversion to account for sample-and-hold errors, then takes a second reading for accuracy. However, as noted by the creator, the HEF4052BP has on-resistance and leakage, so accuracy depends on source impedance and settling time. Channels are read one after another, not simultaneously.
