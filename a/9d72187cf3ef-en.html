OpenMouse: Configure Your Gaming Mouse in the Browser

A free and open-source tool lets you adjust DPI, polling rate, and buttons directly in Chrome or Edge, without sending data externally.

OpenMouse is a browser-based gaming mouse configurator that works in Chrome and Edge. It lets users adjust settings like DPI, polling rate, buttons, and RGB lighting, depending on the mouse model’s capabilities. The tool does not send any data externally and is released under the AGPL license.

Out of 746 tracked devices, only 232 are labeled "Compatible," meaning they are confirmed to work with a dedicated driver. The rest are pending community requests, hardware testing, or driver development. Compatible models include the Logitech G502, MX Master 3S, and Razer DeathAdder V3. Some, like the Corsair Nightsword RGB, are compatible but read-only, requiring the closure of third-party software like iCUE.

Since Chrome 153, a fixed Windows bug blocks direct access to Razer mice via the browser. To bypass this, OpenMouse offers an optional utility called Bridge, which installs on the computer to link the mouse to the web interface. Bridge also supports applying settings profiles at game launch and low-battery alerts for wireless mice. However, Bridge remains in beta and offers no stability guarantees.
