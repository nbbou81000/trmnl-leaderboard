Upgrade Smart Devices with Custom Firmwares

Replace device firmware to unlock hidden features without buying new hardware.

Many consumer smart devices restrict their capabilities through proprietary firmware, but some models include reprogrammable chips like the ESP32 or multi-protocol radios. By replacing their firmware with open-source solutions such as ESPHome, users can add features, remove cloud dependencies, or even switch communication protocols.

Turn a pet feeder into a locally controlled device

The Petkit Fresh Element Solo, an automatic pet food dispenser for cats and dogs, runs on an ESP32. Flashing it with ESPHome makes it fully compatible with Home Assistant without relying on the manufacturer's cloud. The configuration adds manual controls, portion settings, and local scheduling. The process requires opening the device and using a USB-to-serial adapter, but users report success when following instructions tailored to their circuit board revision.

Upgrade a smart plug to an advanced monitoring tool

The Shelly Plug US Gen4, a Wi-Fi smart plug with power monitoring, can be flashed with ESPHome via an OTA update from a browser. The new firmware exposes detailed metrics (power, energy, current, voltage, frequency) and lets you control the RGB LED ring to visualize consumption or connection status. Shelly provides pre-built firmware for several of its products, but this modification removes original features and must be done at your own risk.

Convert a Bluetooth sensor into a Zigbee device

The Xiaomi Mijia Smart Digital Hygrometer 2, a Bluetooth thermometer and hygrometer, can be transformed into a Zigbee sensor thanks to its multi-protocol 2.4 GHz radio. Replacing its firmware switches it to the IEEE 802.15.4 standard used by Zigbee. Some revisions allow OTA flashing from a browser, while others require opening the device and using a USB-to-UART connector. Compatibility depends on hardware and firmware versions, which are hard to identify before purchase. Once modified, it integrates with Home Assistant via Zigbee2MQTT or ZHA.
