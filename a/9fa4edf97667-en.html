Elegoo M1 Ultra: First Portable 3D Scanner with Dual Technology

Elegoo unveils its first portable 3D scanner, the M1 Ultra, merging blue lasers and infrared structured light for versatile and precise scans.

Elegoo introduces the M1 Ultra, its first portable 3D scanner, designed to operate wirelessly via Wi-Fi or tethered via USB-C to a PC. Unlike some standalone models, it features a 3-inch touchscreen for easier remote operation within Wi-Fi range. The device weighs 511 g with a battery and includes an 8-core 2.0GHz processor.

Dual scanning technologies

The M1 Ultra offers two scanning modes: blue laser scanning and near-infrared (NIR) structured light scanning. The former uses laser lines (crossed, parallel, or single) and requires adhesive markers to stabilize tracking, ideal for uniform mechanical parts. The latter, marker-free, uses infrared light to capture organic shapes or objects with distinct patterns. Both modes support color mapping.

The scan volume ranges from 5 × 5 × 5 mm to 4,000 × 4,000 × 4,000 mm, with a 3D resolution of 0.05–2 mm and volumetric accuracy of 0.02 mm + 0.06 mm/m × length. For transparent, shiny, or black objects, Elegoo recommends using a temporary spray (or powder deodorant) to enhance scan quality.

Accessories and user feedback

The scanner ships with two 4,800mAh USB-C rechargeable batteries, a glass calibration board, adhesive markers, NIR filters for bright environments, a mini tripod, a lanyard, and a cleaning cloth. The protective foam-lined hard case, though bulky, ensures robust storage.

Tests show varying performance depending on the object: blue lasers excel with detailed mechanical parts, while infrared works better for organic shapes or faces. Scans of hair or uniform objects remain challenging due to contrast and geometry limitations. Calibration is quick and intuitive, taking just a few minutes via the Elegoo Scan software.
