Home Assistant Optimizes Electric Heating to Save Energy

A year of Home Assistant-controlled heating cuts costs with basic automations and off-peak electricity.

A fully electric heating system, using oil-filled radiators and smart plugs after replacing old storage heaters, was optimized for a year with Home Assistant. The goal was to cut energy costs while maintaining comfort, without investing in expensive solutions like heat pumps.

Savings came from two key strategies: a discounted electricity rate between 00:30 and 05:30 at $0.10 per kWh, versus $0.60 at other times, and strict automation limiting heating duration. Whenever a smart plug turns on a radiator, a 60-minute countdown starts—enough time to reach a comfortable temperature without waste. An additional automation heats the entire house at 04:30, ensuring rooms are warm at wake-up time while leveraging solar batteries charged overnight at low cost.

Radiators only activate if outdoor temperatures drop below 10 °C, preventing unnecessary heating on mild days. Temperature data comes from both local sensors and online services, while the Alarmo integration disables the system when the home is empty. Three basic automations form the core: general activation at 04:30, downstairs heating at 17:00, and bedroom heating at 20:30. These scripts are easily adjustable, allowing manual override of individual radiators without altering existing rules.

The daily radiator consumption dropped from nearly 10 kWh to 4 or 5 kWh, depending on insulation and weather. Savings are even greater since most usage is covered by solar batteries, recharged during off-peak hours. Smart plugs, the only required hardware, must handle sustained loads to prevent overheating risks.
