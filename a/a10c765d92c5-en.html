3D-Printed Case for LILYGO TTGO Meshtastic T-Beam: Two Designs and Easy Assembly

A 3D-printed case offers two bases and two lids for the LILYGO TTGO Meshtastic T-Beam, with display and orientation options.

The project features a PLA case designed for the LILYGO TTGO Meshtastic T-Beam, a wireless mesh networking module. The kit includes four parts: two bottom bases and two top lids, each to be selected separately. The bases come in two versions: a 50° angled base for better viewing angles, and a 90° flat base to keep the device upright. The angled base may interfere with 433 MHz monopole antennas, for which the flat base is recommended.

The top lids are available with or without a cutout for a 1.33-inch OLED screen, secured with four M2×4mm screws. Each base includes a mount for a 25×25×5mm, 32dB GPS antenna. Assembly uses two M2 screws (5–10mm) to connect the lid to the base. The model uses a 0.16mm layer height, three walls, 15% infill, and weighs about 86 grams. Print time is estimated at 3.3 hours.

Files are available for download on MakerWorld at: https://makerworld.com/en/models/1548346-lilygo-ttgo-meshtastic-t-beam-case.
