TICKEY: ESP32-S3 Color E-Paper Badge Launches via Crowdfunding

ENILINX unveils TICKEY, a color e-paper badge powered by ESP32-S3, perfect for dashboards and visual reminders.

ENILINX, operating as ENIAC, has introduced the TICKEY, a 3.7-inch color e-paper smart badge and desk display powered by an ESP32-S3 microcontroller. The device supports Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and NFC connectivity, enabling users to update the screen from a smartphone without needing constant power to maintain a static image.

The display leverages Modos’ color e-paper technology, delivering over 48 native colors with refresh times as fast as 3 seconds and excellent sunlight readability. Measuring just 3.8mm thick and weighing 45 grams, the TICKEY features an aluminum-alloy body and interchangeable faceplates. Two versions are available: a standard model with 8MB of storage and a Developer Edition with 128MB for more advanced applications.

The TICKEY is designed for various uses, including desk reminders, event identification, digital business cards, and interactive maker projects. It also supports NFC and QR code-based web profile sharing for easy contact exchanges.
