AlgoLaser MK3: A DIY Laser Engraver That Balances Accessibility and Versatility

The AlgoLaser MK3 kit offers a 95% preassembled diode laser engraver, designed for both beginners and experienced makers.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

The AlgoLaser MK3 laser engraver features a gantry-style design with an open 400 × 400 mm frame. Available in 8W, 15W, or 20W configurations, it targets engraving and cutting tasks on wood, leather, slate, and anodized materials. The kit arrives 95% preassembled, requiring only five assembly steps, and includes a built-in touchscreen running AlgoOS.

The touchscreen enables standalone operation, streamlining workflows without a computer. However, the open-frame design lacks a protective enclosure, necessitating dedicated ventilation, eye protection, and smoke management—especially for indoor use. Adding an air pump improves cutting quality, but smoke remains a significant challenge.

Compatibility with open-source software like LightBurn and LaserGRBL provides flexibility for advanced users. Performance varies by power: the 8W model suits light engraving, while 15W and 20W versions handle thicker materials. Despite an intuitive interface, customization options are limited, and 2.4GHz Wi-Fi connectivity may be unreliable for remote control.

The MK3 proves versatile for engraving and cutting thin materials but requires careful mechanical setup to avoid precision issues. Its main strength lies in accessibility, while still offering flexibility for experienced users.
