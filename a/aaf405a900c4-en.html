3D-Printed Enclosure for ESP32-S3 in Pinned and Pinless Versions

A compact, customizable enclosure designed to protect ESP32-S3 boards in both pinned and pinless configurations.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

A new 3D-printed enclosure is now available for ESP32-S3 development boards (44-pin format, including ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 and N16R8 variants). The design supports both pinned and pinless configurations through separate print profiles. The top features a stylized Wi-Fi pattern, adding a tech-inspired touch.

The compact, minimalist design maintains proper heat dissipation and board functionality. Two USB Type-C port cutouts are included for full connector compatibility. Print times are optimized: approximately 39 minutes for the pinned version and 29 minutes for the pinless version, using standard settings (0.2mm layer height, 2 walls, 15% infill, 1 plate).

This project is released under the Digital File License, with commercial use permitted via the designer’s Patreon. It aligns well with the Adafruit ecosystem, particularly for CircuitPython users. Files are available for download on MakerWorld: ESP32-S3 Pin & Pinless Enclosure Model.
