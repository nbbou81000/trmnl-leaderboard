3D-Printed Case for RAK Wisblock 19003 with 18650 Battery and Meshtastic Support

A weather-resistant 3D-printed enclosure for the RAK Wisblock 19003 module, designed for a single 18650 battery and LoRa/Meshtastic compatibility.

A new 3D-printed enclosure is available for the RAK Wisblock 19003 module, designed to house a single 18650 lithium-ion battery. Released under the CC BY-NC 4.0 license, this design is explicitly compatible only with the RAK 19003, despite initial tags referencing the RAK 19007. The enclosure includes a small guard for the SMA antenna connector to prevent damage during handling.

The STL file is optimized for PETG filament, with standard settings: 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 10 % infill, requiring approximately 2.2 hours of print time and resulting in a 47-gram final part. This model is a modified version of the DeMeshe Node’s 19007 case and fits into a series of projects supporting the Meshtastic ecosystem, a LoRa-based long-range communication protocol.

The project files are available for download on MakerWorld. It complements a lineup of enclosures designed for different battery form factors, such as the previously announced RAK19007 case, offering flexibility for various power requirements.
