RFOXiA Uses STM32 to Build Long-Range, Mesh-Capable Bluetooth LE Module

A long-range Bluetooth LE module based on STM32WB07CC challenges LoRa solutions for mesh networking without LoRa.

A Bluetooth LE module optimized for long-range communication

RFOXiA is developing the MultiNav Pro+, a Bluetooth Low Energy module designed to deliver extended range and mesh connectivity without relying on LoRa technology. The system is built around an STMicroelectronics STM32WB07CC microcontroller, paired with a 20 dBm power amplifier and a 12 dB low-noise amplifier. With a 4 dBi omnidirectional antenna, the module achieves up to 2 Mb/s at short range and 125 kb/s at long range, according to tests. These speeds support text, telemetry, control signals, and even voice transmission, but not video.

The module uses a modified Bluetooth LE stack to maintain both a standard connection and a long-range link simultaneously. It leverages the Coded PHY at S=8 mode, achieving a maximum measured range of 4.6 km line of sight between two smartphones, each equipped with a module. RFOXiA suggests it could reach up to 50 km in ideal conditions, such as between two drones in a radio-quiet environment.

Advanced features and availability

The MultiNav Pro+ includes multi-hop mesh networking, an offline chat app, GNSS mapping, a gamepad controller, and real-time sensor data display. The firmware, developed with assistance from a large language model, is released under the Apache 2.0 license on GitHub. More details are available on RFOXiA’s website.
