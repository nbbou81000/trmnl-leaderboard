WT32P4C5-43S: ESP32-P4/C5 Dev Board with Built-in 4.3" Touchscreen

A single-board solution merging ESP32-P4 and ESP32-C5 with a 4.3" touchscreen and advanced wireless features.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

The Wireless-Tag WT32P4C5-43S (model ZX4D30CE405-V1.3) is a development board featuring a built-in 4.3" touchscreen and two Espressif chips: an ESP32-P4 for high-performance tasks and an ESP32-C5 for wireless connectivity. The latter supports dual-band Wi-Fi 6 (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz), Bluetooth LE, and 802.15.4, making it suitable for industrial automation, smart control panels, and edge AI vision terminals.

The integrated touchscreen eliminates the need for an external display. Additional features include a microSD card slot, a 15-pin MIPI CSI camera interface, two USB Type-C ports (one for debugging/JTAG), and a 14-pin GPIO expansion header.

The ESP32-P4 combines a high-performance RISC-V core running up to 400 MHz with AI-specific instructions and a single-precision FPU, alongside a low-power RISC-V core at 40 MHz. Memory includes 768 KB of RAM and 8 KB of zero-wait TCM RAM for the low-power core.
