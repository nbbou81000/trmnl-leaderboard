Intel Unveils Agilex 27 FPGA Family: Up to 17.6M Logic Elements and PCIe 7.0 Support

Intel introduces the Agilex 27 FPGA and SoC family, targeting AI infrastructure, high-speed networking, 6G, and satellite communications.

Intel (Altera) has launched the Agilex 27 FPGA and SoC family, designed for AI infrastructure, high-speed networking, 6G communications, and aerospace/defense applications. The lineup includes 14 models, available as standalone FPGAs or SoC variants with integrated Arm processors.

The chips leverage Altera’s third-generation HyperFlex architecture, claimed to deliver 1.8× better performance per watt than the previous Agilex 7 generation. Logic capacity reaches up to 17.6 million logic elements, while SoC variants feature an Arm Cortex-A720/A520 processor subsystem running at up to 2.3 GHz. The Agilex 27 FPGAs also integrate second-generation Tensor DSP blocks capable of up to 1,211 TOPS INT8 performance, alongside LPDDR6 memory interfaces and PCIe 7.0 controllers.

High-speed interfaces include up to 32 SerDes transceivers at 256 Gbps and hardened Ethernet MAC/PCS/FEC controllers, enabling 1.6 TbE throughput. Security features include a Secure Device Manager (SDM) with secure boot, post-quantum cryptography support aligned with CNSA 2.0, and physical anti-tamper protection. The FPGAs are offered in BGA packages ranging from 27×27 mm to 65×65 mm.

Development is supported by Quartus Prime Pro Edition 26.3, which introduces the QuartusIQ AI assistant to aid in design, debugging, and optimization. Early access to Quartus Prime support for Agilex 27 will begin in November 2026, with engineering samples expected in the second half of 2027. Pricing and detailed specifications for the M-Series (with in-package memory) and Direct RF-Series (featuring integrated data converters) variants have not been disclosed.
