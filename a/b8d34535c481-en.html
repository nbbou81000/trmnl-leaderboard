Seeed Studio XIAO STM32C5: STM32C5 Board with Dual CAN FD and Heated IMU

Seeed Studio unveils a XIAO board powered by a 144 MHz STM32C5 MCU, featuring dual CAN FD controllers and a 6-axis IMU with programmable heating.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Seeed Studio has launched the XIAO STM32C5, its first XIAO development board built around an STMicroelectronics MCU. The board features a 144 MHz STM32C5A3CG Arm Cortex-M33 processor with FPU and DSP instructions, targeting robotics, industrial control, and motion-sensing applications.

It includes two CAN FD controllers (FDCAN1 and FDCAN2), with one connected to an onboard TI TCAN3403-Q1 CAN FD transceiver and the other designed for an external transceiver. A programmable heating circuit surrounds the LSM6DS3TR-C 6-axis IMU (3-axis accelerometer and gyroscope), allowing firmware to regulate sensor temperature and minimize measurement drift caused by thermal variations. The heater, controlled via PWM, can deliver up to 0.58W.

Memory-wise, the board offers 256KB SRAM, 1MB internal flash, and 16MB external flash. It includes a USB-C port (USB 2.0 Full-Speed), a 3.7V Li-ion/LiPo battery charging circuit, and an SWD debugging interface. I/O options cover GPIO, ADC, I2C, SPI, UART, plus dedicated pads for CAN FD interfaces and battery connections. The board measures 21 × 17.8 mm, fitting the XIAO form factor.

Seeed Studio provides development resources for STM32CubeIDE, PlatformIO (with Zephyr RTOS), and MicroPython, along with CAN FD and IMU examples. Hardware files (schematics, KiCad/Eagle designs) and software resources are available on the dedicated wiki.
