Orange Pi O1: A 16 TOPS SBC for AI and Video Processing

A new single-board computer integrates a SOPHGO BM1688 processor with a 16 TOPS AI accelerator and 4G/5G connectivity.

Orange Pi has unveiled the O1, a single-board computer (SBC) targeting high-performance computing and video processing. At its core lies the SOPHGO BM1688 processor, featuring eight Cortex-A53 cores running at 1.65 GHz, paired with a RISC-V C906 coprocessor at 1.2 GHz. The SoC includes a Neural Processing Unit (TPU) delivering up to 16 TOPS in INT8 precision, suitable for running local AI models such as Gemma-2B, Qwen1.5-1.8B, Llama2-7B, or ChatGLM3-6B.

The O1 supports up to sixteen 1080p30 video streams for decoding or ten for encoding, thanks to its dedicated VPU. It also handles JPEG decoding and encoding at 480 frames per second in 1080p. Storage options include 8GB or 16GB LPDDR4/LPDDR4X RAM, eMMC modules ranging from 16GB to 128GB, an M.2 M-Key socket for NVMe SSDs, a SATA III port, or a microSD card slot. For display output, it offers an HDMI 2.0 port (up to 4Kp60) and a 4-lane MIPI DSI connector, along with two MIPI CSI interfaces for cameras.

Connectivity features include dual Gigabit Ethernet ports, Wi-Fi 6E, and Bluetooth 5.3. An optional 4G/5G module can be added via an M.2 B-Key socket and SIM card slot. USB ports include two USB 3.0 and two USB 2.0, while a 26-pin GPIO header allows for expansions. Power is supplied via a USB Type-C port (up to 20V/3.25A), and an active cooler is required. Pricing starts at $195 (8GB RAM) or $325 (16GB RAM), with the cooler priced at $7.9 extra. Both models are available on AliExpress.
