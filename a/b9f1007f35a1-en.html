M5Stack Paper Mono: ESP32-S3 E-Paper Dev Board with 3.97-inch Touchscreen, LoRa, and NFC

M5Stack has launched a new ESP32-S3-based e-paper development board featuring a 3.97-inch monochrome touchscreen, LoRa, and NFC connectivity.

M5Stack has expanded its e-paper development board lineup with the Paper Mono, a new ESP32-S3-based board featuring a 3.97-inch monochrome touchscreen display. Designed for faster refresh rates than its predecessor, the PaperColor, this kit targets projects requiring responsive e-paper interfaces.

The board includes 8MB of PSRAM, 16MB of flash storage, a microSD card slot, and audio capabilities with a PDM microphone and buzzer. Additional features include a 6-axis IMU for motion tracking, an RTC for timekeeping, and physical buttons alongside an RGB LED for status indication. Connectivity options cover 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 4, Bluetooth 5.0 LE (including Mesh), LoRa for long-range communication, and NFC for short-range interactions.

Powered through a USB-C port and a 1150 mAh battery, the Paper Mono is available in two variants: a standard version with LoRa and NFC, and a Lite version without these features. The board does not include any expansion ports.
