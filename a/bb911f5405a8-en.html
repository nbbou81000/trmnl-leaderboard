10.1-inch Touchscreen Display for Arduino UNO Q with Speakers and Audio Jacks

A 10.1-inch touchscreen display featuring built-in speakers and audio jacks is now available for the Arduino UNO Q single-board computer.

Waveshare has launched a 10.1-inch touchscreen display designed specifically for the Arduino UNO Q single-board computer. The UNO-Q-DSI-TOUCH-10.1A model connects directly via the board’s JMEDIA and JMISC headers, removing the need for an additional adapter like the Arduino UNO Media Carrier. The display features an IPS panel with a native resolution of 1280 x 800 pixels, a 60 Hz refresh rate, and a 178° viewing angle. Capacitive multi-touch is supported, though the exact number of touch points depends on the operating system.

Audio features include built-in stereo speakers and two 3.5 mm audio jacks: one for audio input and microphone, and another for audio output only. Two MIPI CSI connectors allow for camera modules, compatible exclusively with IMX219 sensors. An RGB LED connector and a metal backplate are also included. Power is delivered via a USB-C port requiring 5V at 3A.

Installation is straightforward, but users must ensure correct alignment and orientation of the connectors. Waveshare provides detailed documentation for hardware and software setup, including enabling carrier board support in Arduino App Lab. This display is only compatible with the Arduino UNO Q and not the VENTUNO Q model.

The UNO-Q-DSI-TOUCH-10.1A is priced at $69.99 on Waveshare’s store and $80.09 on AliExpress. Bundles including the display and Arduino UNO Q (in 2GB/16GB or 4GB/32GB variants) start at $120. Availability on Amazon is expected at a later date.
