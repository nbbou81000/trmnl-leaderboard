XIMEA Unveils Miniature USB 3.0 ToF Sensor with 60 FPS Support

A compact 0.3MP industrial ToF module designed for embedded vision and robotics applications.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

XIMEA has introduced the MU003TG-SY-UC, a miniature industrial Time-of-Flight (ToF) camera. It features a 0.3-megapixel Sony IMX556 CMOS sensor (640 × 480 pixels) with a global shutter, delivering a 60 FPS frame rate and a pixel size of 10 µm × 10 µm.

The module’s compact dimensions (26.4 × 26.4 × 28.67 mm) and 31-gram weight make it suitable for space-constrained embedded vision systems, including robotics, UAVs, interactive kiosks, 3D scanning, and face recognition. It connects via a USB 3.2 Gen 1 interface using a 14-pin I-PEX Cabline SS connector for power, data, and control. A separate 10-pin Cabline V connector enables synchronization with external VCSEL illumination.

Notably, the camera does not include an illumination source or optical band-pass filter, which must be provided separately for depth-sensing applications. XIMEA states the module is designed for embedded vision systems requiring compact, high-performance integration.
