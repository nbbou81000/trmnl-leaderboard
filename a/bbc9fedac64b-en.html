An industrial robot arm learns to juggle, a precision challenge

A uFactory robot arm takes on the challenge of juggling balls, a project born from a hackathon.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

As part of the Fine Motor Skills Hackathon organized by Viam in September 2026, a team including Mandy Meindersma, Jie Xuan Tang, and Aika K attempted to make a uFactory industrial robot arm juggle balls. Known for its precision, the arm was tested on fast, ballistic motions typical of juggling.

The project, though simple in concept, proved far more challenging than expected. The team had to develop a specific algorithm to synchronize the arm’s movements with the throwing and catching of the balls. The open-source code, available on Hackster.io and the associated GitHub repository, provides insights into the techniques used to control the arm’s trajectory and speed.

The results are shared on these platforms, offering a foundation for further improvements or adaptations. This project highlights the current limitations of industrial robots when faced with tasks requiring fine coordination and real-time responsiveness.
