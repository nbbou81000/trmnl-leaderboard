An FPGA Recreation of the 1985 AT&T UNIX PC for MiSTer

A hobbyist recreates the 1985 AT&T UNIX PC in FPGA, compatible with MiSTer, to explore this rare and powerful system.

The AT&T UNIX PC, released in 1985, was a bulky workstation (18 kg) featuring a Motorola 68010 CPU at 10 MHz, up to 4 MB of RAM, and several dozen megabytes of storage. Designed for professionals, it offered extensive expansion options but never gained mainstream success, overshadowed by IBM PC and Apple systems.

Decades later, Hrvoje Cavrak has created an FPGA version of this machine, allowing it to run on MiSTer-compatible boards, such as the DE10 Nano. The project uses components written in SystemVerilog, Verilog, and VHDL, and requires an SDRAM expansion board for the DE10 Nano, as its integrated FPGA memory cannot accommodate the required 4 MB.

To function, the project needs the boot ROM and disk images of AT&T UNIX, which remain under copyright. While these files are available online, their use may raise legal concerns. Once set up, the emulator delivers a complete UNIX PC experience, as demonstrated in a video.

Parts of the code were written and validated by AI coding assistants, making this a work-in-progress project (« works on my MiSTer board »). For those unable to obtain the original hardware—now priced at unobtanium levels—this FPGA solution offers a fair alternative.
