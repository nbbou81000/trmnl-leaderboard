Fluffpad: A Retro MicroPython Game Console for RP2350

A pocket retro console built on a Raspberry Pi RP2350, designed for family game coding with MicroPython.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Ilya Titov, known as Webgoggles, has built Fluffpad, a pocket retro console powered by a Raspberry Pi RP2350. The project is a family affair, developed alongside his daughter Maya. Games are coded in MicroPython and draw inspiration from 8-bit consoles like the NES, Sega Master System, and home computers of the era.

The build uses Pimoroni components, including a display pack and a Qw/ST pad, assembled with hot glue and Lego pieces. Three games are already available: Fluffbert’s Frozen Dream, La-Fluff-Puff Skedaddle, and La-Fluff-Puff and the Jade Sun. A platformer bootstrap is also included, allowing users to create a simple one-room platform game.

Assembly and development files are shared on GitHub. Additional details can be found on the Pimoroni blog.
