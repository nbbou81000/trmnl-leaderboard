Claude Fable 5.2 Designs Complete Electronic Prototypes from Scratch

AI designs complete electronic prototypes, including circuits, firmware, and enclosures, without human input.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Anthropic has announced that its latest model, Claude Fable 5.2, developed in partnership with Diode Computers, can now design complete electronic prototypes from start to finish. Starting with a simple text prompt, the AI generates technical specifications, draws electronic schematics, places components on a printed circuit board (PCB), routes copper traces, designs a 3D-printable enclosure, writes firmware, and compiles a parts list.

The model iteratively refines its own work, correcting errors such as untangling and rerouting poorly placed copper traces. For a voice-assistant hub, it identified that KiCad’s built-in router required too much manual guidance for long traces from a 100-pin connector, so it wrote a custom grid-search router in about 30 minutes. It also performs manufacturing and parts-sourcing checks, distinguishing real issues from non-critical flags.

On Diode’s internal PCB Gym benchmark, Claude Fable 5.2 scored 69%, compared to 25% for the previous version. On the public BenchCAD 2.1 benchmark, it achieved 83.5%. Diode manufactured three prototypes—a USB-C speakerphone puck, a voice-assistant hub, and a handheld virtual pet—directly from the generated design files without any modifications. These prototypes have not yet undergone electrical validation, antenna tuning, thermal testing, or certification.

Design files and technical details are available on GitHub and Anthropic’s research blog.
