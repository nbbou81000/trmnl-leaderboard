Arduino Nano XL Link: A Powerful Arduino Nano Variant for Industrial and Automation Projects

Arduino unveils an upgraded Nano board featuring a Microchip PIC32CK Cortex-M33 MCU at 120 MHz, expanded memory, and industrial interfaces.

Arduino has introduced the Arduino Nano XL Link, an upgraded version of its popular Nano board, maintaining the same compact dimensions (43 x 23 mm) but featuring a Microchip PIC32CK Cortex-M33 MCU running at 120 MHz. This MCU offers 2 MB of flash memory and 512 KB of RAM, compared to just 32 KB of flash and 2 KB of RAM on the original Nano. It also includes a Hardware Security Module (HSM) and TrustZone technology for secure applications.

The Nano XL Link is designed for industrial, home, and building automation, thanks to advanced communication interfaces: a CAN FD transceiver (up to 8 Mbps), an RS-485 transceiver (500 kbps), and a 10/100 Mbps Ethernet controller. These interfaces are accessible via J3 and J4 headers, with onboard 120 Ω termination resistors that can be disabled by soldering. The board also provides 26 digital I/O pins, 8 12-bit analog inputs, 8 PWM outputs, and a Qwiic connector for I2C expansion.

To simplify prototyping, Arduino offers the Nano XL Link Connector Carrier, a carrier board that exposes industrial interfaces via screw terminals (CAN FD, RS-485, RJ45 Ethernet) and adds an ESP-01 form factor Wi-Fi connector. The carrier is compatible with standard DIN-rail enclosures and supports a 7 to 24 V power supply. Both products were unveiled at Electronica 2026, but are not yet available for purchase, with no pricing or availability details provided.
