Sipeed Launches SLogic32U3, a 1.4 GS/s Logic Analyzer for $149

Sipeed is crowdfunding the SLogic32U3, a third-generation USB 3.2 logic analyzer with up to 1.4 GS/s sampling rate, on Kickstarter.

Sipeed, a Chinese embedded and DIY electronics specialist, is crowdfunding the SLogic32U3, a third-generation USB 3.2 logic analyzer. The device delivers up to 10 Gb/s throughput and a maximum sampling rate of 1.4 GS/s on four channels. Performance scales with channel count: 800 MS/s on eight channels, 400 MS/s on sixteen, and 200 MHz on thirty-two.

The SLogic32U3 improves upon previous models like the SLogic16U3 by doubling the streaming rate (up to 800 MB/s) and adding 256 MB of DDR3 buffer memory. The maximum input frequency rises from 200 MHz to 350 MHz, while the input voltage range remains 0–10 V with an adjustable 0–6 V logic trigger threshold.

A new feature in this generation is an optional analog-to-digital converter (ADC) module, adding four 8-bit channels at 100 MS/s for $15. The analyzer is compatible with open-source software like ngscopeclient and includes a cross-platform web app running on Linux, Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, and other systems with a modern USB-capable browser. The app features over 150 protocol decoders from the Sigrok project.

Funding for the SLogic32U3 is live on Kickstarter, with physical rewards starting at $149 for a single unit. Shipments are expected in November 2026, though crowdfunding risks remain despite Sipeed’s track record.
