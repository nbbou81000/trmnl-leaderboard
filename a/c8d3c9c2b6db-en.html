LEDVANCE Joins Home Assistant Ecosystem with 116 Certified Devices

LED lighting manufacturer LEDVANCE joins the Works with Home Assistant program with 116 Matter and Wi-Fi compatible devices.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Founded in 2016 as LEDVANCE after OSRAM’s lighting division split into an independent entity, this German manufacturer has grown into a global player with offices in over 50 countries. Its products, sold under the LEDVANCE and OSRAM brands through a licensing agreement, combine over a century of lighting expertise with modern technologies like the Matter standard.

The 116 certified Works with Home Assistant devices include filament bulbs, spotlights, recessed and track lights, ceiling and wall lamps, flexible LED strips, and outdoor lighting such as floodlights and weatherproof spots. The lineup also features smart plugs to automate existing appliances and smart wall switches, allowing users to keep their current bulbs while adding smart control.

All these devices use the Matter protocol over Wi-Fi, ensuring local integration without relying on external cloud services. This approach enhances privacy and long-term reliability, as the devices remain functional even if a platform changes or a remote service shuts down. Certified products have been tested to ensure local control and lasting compatibility with Home Assistant.

The Matter compatibility also enables interoperability between brands, preventing lock-in to a single ecosystem. LEDVANCE’s devices offer features like tunable white lighting (from warm to cool tones) or RGB effects, along with options for damp or outdoor environments. Their certification also highlights a commitment to sustainability, with devices designed to remain functional over time without planned obsolescence.

Users can find the full list of certified devices on the dedicated page. LEDVANCE has also announced ongoing collaboration with Home Assistant to integrate additional products in the future.
