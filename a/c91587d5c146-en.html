Inkolor Frame: A Magnetic, Battery-Powered Color E-Ink Smart Display

A battery-powered, offline-first color e-ink smart display featuring swappable magnetic frames and a custom rendering engine for better artwork reproduction.

Reeveel, an art-focused collective, has unveiled the Inkolor Frame, a battery-powered smart display using color e-ink technology from E Ink. Designed to showcase artwork or photographs, it prioritizes an offline-first approach, storing images on a microSD card while offering optional Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity for MQTT updates or mobile app integration. Widgets like weather, calendar, and quotes are also supported.

A key innovation is its magnetic frame system: the visible frame and mat can be detached and swapped without touching the display. The wall mount also allows automatic rotation between portrait and landscape modes. To address the limitations of color e-ink (narrow gamut, low contrast), Reeveel developed PaperTone, a custom rendering engine that adjusts colors, contrast, and fine details to match the display’s capabilities.

The 13.3-inch model features an E Ink Spectra 6 panel (1,200 × 1,600 pixels, 150 ppi) capable of displaying six primary colors. Through dithering, PaperTone simulates a broader color range. Powered by a rechargeable USB-C battery, it claims up to one year of battery life with three image changes per day. The frame is currently available on Kickstarter for CA$399 (around $282), a 30% discount off the planned retail price. Shipments to backers are expected in April 2027, though crowdfunding fulfillment risks remain.
