TOOCAA NOVA: 20W Laser Engraver with AI Camera and Rotary Module

A compact 20W laser engraver featuring an AI camera, autofocus, and a rotary module for cylindrical and flat objects.

The TOOCAA NOVA is a desktop laser cutter and engraver designed for creative projects such as signs, decorations, or personalized gifts. Its compact design includes an A4-sized work area (297 × 210 mm) and a translucent orange cover. The reviewed model features a 20W diode laser module, compatible with materials like wood, paper, leather, and coated aluminum.

The machine stands out with its 5MP AI camera, integrated into the control software, which displays a real-time image of the work area. This feature simplifies aligning designs before engraving or cutting. An autofocus system automatically adjusts the focal distance, while a material identification card system helps select optimal settings for each material. The machine operates with TOOCAA Studio software, which handles design creation, image import, and job configuration (line engraving, fill engraving, or cutting).

The optional rotary module enables engraving cylindrical objects like glasses or tumblers. The machine’s X-axis rotates 90 degrees to accommodate this module, which includes a three-jaw chuck and a belt-driven motor system. A 2.4-inch touchscreen controls the machine and displays its status. Multiple safety features are built-in, including automatic shutdown when the cover is opened, flame detection, tilt detection, and a child lock.

The TOOCAA NOVA engraver ships with accessories including the 20W laser module, air assist pump, rotary module, cables, and a toolkit. Sample materials (basswood, kraft paper, aluminum tags) are included for initial testing. The 20W diode laser head, classified as Class 4, requires use with the cover closed. The power supply delivers 24V DC with a maximum output of 180W.
