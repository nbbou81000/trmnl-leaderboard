Sound-Responsive Glowing Hair for a Rapunzel Cosplay

A DIY cosplay accessory turns a Rapunzel wig into sound-reactive glowing hair using a Circuit Playground Bluefruit.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

This Halloween cosplay project transforms a Rapunzel wig into a sound-reactive glowing accessory. The Circuit Playground Bluefruit—an Adafruit board with a built-in microphone—listens for voice or music and triggers a golden light animation. The integrated LEDs pulse in waves, mimicking the magical glow of Rapunzel’s hair from Disney’s Tangled.

The build is beginner-friendly, though it requires some careful wiring. The included guide features ready-to-use CircuitPython code, making it easy to customize the animation’s color and timing. This project is ideal for kid-friendly workshops, allowing participants to adapt the design to their favorite characters.
