Seeed Studio Unveils XIAO IPS Displays with ESP32-S3 or nRF52840

Seeed Studio launches six compact development boards featuring color IPS displays and wireless connectivity.

Seeed Studio has expanded its XIAO lineup with the XIAO IPS display series, a set of six compact development boards featuring color IPS displays and wireless connectivity. Each board integrates either an ESP32-S3 Plus or nRF52840 Plus microcontroller, paired with a 0.96-inch, 1.14-inch, or 1.47-inch IPS color display. All models include a 6-axis IMU, a PDM digital microphone, user buttons, and LiPo battery support.

The 1.47-inch variant stands out with capacitive touch and a microSD card slot, while the 1.14-inch model adds a Grove I2C connector for external sensors. ESP32-S3 versions support Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE, making them suitable for connected dashboards or IoT projects. The nRF52840 variants focus on low-power Bluetooth LE applications, ideal for wearables and portable devices.

Performance-wise, the ESP32-S3 features a dual-core processor up to 240 MHz with 8MB RAM and 16MB flash, while the nRF52840 offers a single-core 64 MHz design with 1MB flash and 256KB RAM. Displays use ST7789 controllers (0.96-inch and 1.14-inch) or JD9853A (1.47-inch). The boards are programmable via the Arduino IDE, with dedicated graphics libraries and examples for audio, IMU, and microSD storage. Additional resources, including schematics, KiCad files, and 3D models, are available on Seeed Studio’s Wiki.

Priced between $11.99 and $20.33 on AliExpress and $15.90 and $19.90 on Seeed Studio’s official store, these boards target embedded projects, smart home controllers, and portable devices requiring compact, color displays.
