Home Assistant Connect AUX-2: A $69 Hub to Modernize Your Old Devices

A new $69 box turns non-connected speakers and TVs into smart devices via Home Assistant.

Nabu Casa expands its hardware lineup with the Home Assistant Connect AUX-2, a compact hub designed to integrate non-connected electronic devices into the Home Assistant ecosystem. The device stands out for its ability to stream audio to speakers, amplifiers, or TVs via analog and optical outputs while maintaining lossless CD-quality playback. It supports local files and compatible services through the free Music Assistant software, though it lacks built-in audio input or amplification.

The hub also features two RS-232 serial ports to locally control compatible smart TVs or AV receivers without relying on the internet. This allows users to disable network connectivity on these devices while still managing them via Home Assistant, though built-in streaming apps will no longer be accessible. Other equipment, such as climate systems or solar inverters, can be connected using a separate USB RS-485 adapter, provided a dedicated Home Assistant integration exists.

The device also functions as a Bluetooth proxy, enabling nearby Bluetooth smart home devices to connect directly to Home Assistant without complex setup. Additionally, it supports Power over Ethernet (PoE), allowing both power and network connectivity through a single Ethernet cable, simplifying installation. The Home Assistant Connect AUX-2 is priced at $69 and requires Home Assistant 2026.9 or later, configured via the ESPHome integration.
