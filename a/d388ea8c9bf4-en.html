New Adafruit BNO055+BMP280 Sensor Module for QT Py

A combined sensor module featuring accelerometer, gyroscope and barometer, compatible with QT Py boards, is showcased in Adafruit’s weekly live stream.

The Adafruit BNO055 + BMP280 BFF module is designed to fit QT Py development boards, a compact and cost-effective platform. It combines two sensors: the BNO055, which integrates an accelerometer, gyroscope and magnetometer for 9-axis orientation tracking, and the BMP280, a precise barometer for atmospheric pressure and altitude measurements.

This module is ideal for projects requiring advanced motion detection or environmental monitoring, such as navigation systems, weather stations or position tracking devices. Its compatibility with QT Py boards streamlines integration into electronic prototypes through standard pin connections.

The module was showcased live by John Park during the October 20, 2026 edition of JP’s Product Pick of the Week, featuring a real-time demonstration of its capabilities. Viewers learned how to process its data using dedicated software libraries compatible with Arduino and CircuitPython environments.
