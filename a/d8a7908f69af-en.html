DEBIX M8391-01 Industrial SBC Packs 9-TOPS NPU and Dual-Display Support

A compact single-board computer for edge AI and computer vision, featuring a 9-TOPS NPU and dual-display outputs.

The DEBIX M8391-01 is a credit-card-sized industrial single-board computer powered by the MediaTek Genio 720 (MT8391) processor. At its core, the board integrates a 9-TOPS NPU for local AI inference without cloud dependency. It supports up to 16GB of LPDDR4 RAM and 64GB of eMMC storage, expandable via an M.2 PCIe 2.0 NVMe slot and a MicroSD card reader.

For video interfaces, the SBC offers a 4-lane MIPI CSI camera input, a MIPI DSI display output up to 2.5K resolution, and an eDP 1.4 display interface. Connectivity features include Gigabit Ethernet with PoE, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.4, five USB ports, and a 40-pin expansion header. Designed for industrial environments, it operates reliably from -40°C to 85°C.

Targeting edge AI, computer vision, robotics, and industrial IoT applications, this compact board serves as a versatile solution for demanding embedded systems.
