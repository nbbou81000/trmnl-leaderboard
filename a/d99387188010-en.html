Everything Smart Home Joins Works with Home Assistant with Presence Sensors

Everything Smart Home joins the Works with Home Assistant program with three ESPHome presence sensors, prioritizing privacy and millimeter-wave detection.

Everything Smart Home, a UK-based company founded by Lewis Barclay and his father Dave (an electrical engineer), has joined the Works with Home Assistant program with its Everything Presence sensor lineup. These devices, built on ESPHome, integrate seamlessly with Home Assistant without relying on the cloud, ensuring local control and data privacy.

Three models are available: the Lite, One, and Pro. The Lite uses millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar to track up to three people across four customizable zones. The One adds temperature, humidity, light sensing, and PIR motion detection for instant responsiveness. The Pro, the most advanced model, combines two mmWave radars (long-range static and multi-target tracking), PIR, and light sensing, with Power over Ethernet (PoE, 802.3af) for professional installations without Wi-Fi or power outlet concerns.

All sensors operate locally via ESPHome, featuring open-source code and the Made for ESPHome certification, confirming their plug-and-play compatibility. Their active community contributes to improvements via GitHub, and Lewis Barclay has participated in events like the Open Home Foundation Summit to engage with users. Long-term software updates and support are guaranteed by the manufacturer.

These sensors stand out for their millimeter-wave detection, capable of sensing subtle movements like breathing, unlike traditional PIR sensors that miss stationary occupants. They also act as Bluetooth proxies, extending the network range in a home. Certified models undergo testing for local control, privacy compliance, and sustainability.
