SparkFun Launches Two ESP32-P4 Boards for IoT, with Ethernet or Wi-Fi 6

SparkFun unveils two development boards based on Espressif's ESP32-P4, one with Ethernet PoE and the other with an ESP32-C5 coprocessor for Wi-Fi 6 and Zigbee.

SparkFun has announced two new RedBoard development boards designed for the Internet of Things (IoT): the IoT RedBoard ESP32-P4 and the IoT RedBoard ESP32-P4 ESP32-C5. Both are built around Espressif’s ESP32-P4 microcontroller, a high-end chip featuring two 32-bit RISC-V cores running up to 400 MHz, a low-power core at 40 MHz, 768 KB of RAM for the high-performance cores, 32 MB of pseudo-static RAM (PSRAM), and 16 MB of external flash with microSD support.

The IoT RedBoard ESP32-P4 offers wired Fast Ethernet connectivity via an RJ45 port with Power-over-Ethernet (PoE) support. The IoT RedBoard ESP32-P4 ESP32-C5 replaces Ethernet with an ESP32-C5 coprocessor for wireless communications, including dual-band 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5 Low Energy (BLE), and IEEE 802.15.4 protocols such as Zigbee and Thread. Both boards include a Qwiic I2C connector, two USB Type-C ports, a JST battery connector, and MIPI CSI and DSI interfaces for cameras and displays.

Both models are available for purchase on SparkFun’s store, priced at $55.95 for the Ethernet version and $49.95 for the wireless version, before volume discounts.
