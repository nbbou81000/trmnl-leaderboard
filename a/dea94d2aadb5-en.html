Build a Time-Lapse Camera with a Raspberry Pi 4

An open-source project turns a Raspberry Pi 4 into a self-sufficient time-lapse camera to document a garden renovation.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

A maker built a self-sufficient time-lapse camera to document each step of a garden renovation. The system uses a Raspberry Pi 4 paired with a Camera Module 3 Wide (12 MP), powered through a single PoE (Power over Ethernet) cable.

The open-source software, available on GitHub, captures a full 12MP photo every minute during daylight hours, adjusting intervals based on geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) to account for seasonal changes. When the SD card reaches over 85% capacity, the oldest frames are automatically deleted to free up space. A Mac can retrieve the images whenever an external drive is connected, and a single command generates a video from any selected date range. The project also includes a film mode and a smartphone-compatible control interface.

Source files and technical details are shared on GitHub, YouTube, and Reddit. A step-by-step guide is in development.
