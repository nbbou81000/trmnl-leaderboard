Sprout: Upgrade Your Old iPod with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and 2 TB of Storage

A full logic board replacement turns 3rd/4th-gen iPods and mini models into modern devices while preserving their iconic design.

The Sprout project offers a radical solution to breathe new life into 3rd and 4th generation iPods, as well as 1st and 2nd generation iPod mini models. Instead of merely replacing the hard drive with flash storage or upgrading the battery, this modification replaces the entire logic board while keeping the original case, monochrome screen, click wheel, and headphone jack intact. The electronics are completely overhauled.

The new board features a Raspberry Pi RP2350 microcontroller, a Cirrus Logic CS43131 DAC for high-fidelity audio output, and modern connectivity options: Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and USB-C. Storage capacity can reach up to 2 TB via a microSD card, and playback supports MP3, AAC, ALAC, FLAC, and Opus formats. Files are synced simply by dragging and dropping over USB-C, bypassing iTunes. Bluetooth enables wireless headphones like AirPods, while the 3.5 mm headphone jack remains functional for wired use.

Two versions of the board are available. The Sprout Pico, based solely on the RP2350, focuses on local music and podcast playback with instant startup. It is compatible with 3G and 4G iPods and both generations of iPod mini. The Sprout Pi adds a Raspberry Pi Compute Module Zero alongside the RP2350, providing enough processing power for custom apps and advanced network features like Spotify, Apple Music, podcast streaming, AirPlay, and Google Cast. Prototypes of these features are already functional, but the developers caution that third-party API changes could affect their availability at launch.

Battery life is targeted at around 40 hours of continuous Bluetooth playback for 3G and 4G iPods (2,500 mAh battery) and 20 hours for iPod mini models (1,200 mAh battery). The entire project is open source, including schematics, PCB layouts, firmware, software, and reverse-engineering documentation for the iPod display. Pricing and release date have not been announced, but a waitlist is open on Crowd Supply.
