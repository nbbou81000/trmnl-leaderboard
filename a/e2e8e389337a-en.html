Recovery Kit 3: A Portable Cyberdeck with Dual Batteries for Off-Grid Use

Jay Doscher unveils the Recovery Kit 3, a rugged cyberdeck featuring a 13.3" AMOLED display and dual V-Mount batteries for off-grid operation.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Jay Doscher, an experienced maker, has introduced the Recovery Kit 3, the latest iteration of his portable cyberdeck series designed for off-grid use. This model stands out with the addition of dual V-Mount batteries, enabling extended autonomous operation without relying on external power sources.

The Recovery Kit 3 is built around a modified Pelican Storm iM2200 case, adapted to house a 13.3-inch AMOLED display as its lid. The Waveshare-supplied screen includes integrated USB, Ethernet, and GPIO connectors at the base of the case. The system is powered by a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B, though Doscher notes the modular design could easily accommodate a Raspberry Pi 5 or another embedded system.

Power is provided by two 99 Wh V-Mount batteries, one for the display and one for the Raspberry Pi. These removable and lockable batteries offer sufficient runtime for off-grid use, though they are not hot-swappable—removing a battery cuts power immediately to its connected component. The internal framework combines laser-cut aluminum parts and 3D-printed elements, designed to fit the case’s dimensions while remaining lightweight and functional.

The project is fully documented on Doscher’s blog, including a bill of materials and design files available to paid subscribers.
