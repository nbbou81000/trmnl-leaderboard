Prusa Unveils High-Temperature Hotend for CORE One Printers

Prusa introduces a high-temperature hotend (up to 400°C) for its CORE One printers, alongside dedicated filaments.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Prusa Research, a leader in 3D printing, has unveiled a high-temperature hotend (HT Hotend) compatible with its CORE One line of printers, including the CORE One, CORE One L, CORE One+, CORE One L+, and CORE One+ Gen. 2. This accessory enables printing temperatures up to 400°C, compared to the 300°C limit of standard hotends.

The HT Hotend is marketed as a cost-effective solution for printing high-performance filaments, which are often expensive and typically require industrial-grade machines. Prusa claims its $199.99 price tag is a fraction of the $10,000 cost of comparable industrial equipment. Installation is quick, taking less than a minute to swap out a standard hotend, though it is not compatible with the INDX automatic material-changing system.

Performance gains include improved interlayer adhesion, with Prusa reporting a 7% to 23% increase depending on the material used. Compatible filaments include Prusaments PA11 Carbon Fiber, PC Blend Carbon Fiber, PC Blend, and ASA, optimized for temperatures above 300°C. Three new third-party filaments are also available: Polymaker Fiberon PPS-CF, PPS-GF, and Siraya Tech PPA-CF Core, known for their thermal and chemical resistance.

The HT Hotend is now available for purchase on the Prusa store for $199.99, and is also offered as a factory-fitted option on the CORE One L+ printer, starting at $2,169. The new filaments are priced from $72.99 for a 1kg spool of Polymaker Fiberon PPS‑GF20.
