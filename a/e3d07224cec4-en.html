A $48 e-paper display to show whatever you want, no subscription required

A $48 DIY kit merges an e-paper screen with flexible firmware to display calendars, weather or custom feeds.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Seeed Studio offers a $48 DIY kit featuring a 7.5-inch e-paper display (800 × 480 pixels) and a control board based on an ESP32-S3 Plus. Developed in partnership with TRMNL, the device can display custom information feeds through three different software ecosystems. The kit includes a 2000 mAh rechargeable battery, a Wi-Fi antenna, an FPC cable and connector, and two 3D-printable case designs (triangular prism or L-shape).

Three setup options are available. The first uses TRMNL’s BYOD ecosystem (bring your own device), with a 14-day free trial followed by a $50 license to connect the device to their servers. The second relies on standalone software solutions, such as integration with Home Assistant or Seeed’s SenseCraft platform, which provides a workspace and a plugin repository. Advanced users can host their own server using the open-source Terminus container, ensuring private data management.

The monochrome, flicker-free e-paper screen refreshes automatically to display information like weather, calendars, or custom feeds. The firmware even allows creating HTML-based plugins rendered as bitmaps. Assembling the kit requires caution: the FPC cable is extremely fragile and must be handled carefully to avoid breaks, especially when installing it in an L-shaped case where the cable must navigate a tight angle. Seeed recommends the prismatic model to simplify cable routing.
