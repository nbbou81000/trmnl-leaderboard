M5Stack ToughC5: Rugged ESP32-C5 IoT Controller with Wi-Fi 6, BLE 5, and Zigbee/Thread

M5Stack unveils the ToughC5, a rugged IoT controller for outdoor use, powered by ESP32-C5 with Wi-Fi 6, BLE 5, and Zigbee/Thread support.

M5Stack has launched the ToughC5, a rugged IoT controller designed for outdoor environments. The device is powered by Espressif’s ESP32-C5 microcontroller, delivering advanced connectivity with dual-band Wi-Fi 6 (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz), Bluetooth 5 LE, and an IEEE 802.15.4 radio supporting Zigbee and Thread protocols.

The ToughC5 features a 2.0-inch capacitive touch IPS display and an RS485 port for industrial communications. It succeeds the ESP32-based M5Stack TOUGH released in 2021, with significant upgrades including faster Octal PSRAM, an upgraded RTC chip with a dedicated coin cell backup battery, and a new power management system (M5PM1 + M5IOE1) replacing the previous AXP192 PMU. A passive buzzer replaces the former 1W speaker.

This controller is tailored for demanding applications such as industrial field control, outdoor edge data collection, and smart building systems. Pricing and availability details have not been disclosed yet.
