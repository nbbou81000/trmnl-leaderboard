E Ink Kaleido 4: The Next-Gen Color E-Paper Arrives

E Ink unveils Kaleido 4, a color e-paper platform offering brighter contrast, smoother video up to 18 FPS, and wider color gamut.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

E Ink, a leader in electrophoretic display technology, has launched Kaleido 4, its fourth-generation color e-paper platform. This update delivers major improvements in color accuracy, brightness and contrast, enabling smoother rendering of diverse content—PDFs, presentations, charts, and even video—with reduced graininess.

Key technical enhancements include a doubled color gamut volume (over 40,000 colors), a 15% increase in white reflectance, and a 50% boost in contrast. New algorithms now support video playback at up to 18 frames per second, a first for color e-paper. These upgrades aim to meet the demands of professional and educational settings where screens must accurately display complex documents.

First device featuring Kaleido 4: Hanvon’s M10C II

The M10C II, a digital notebook from Hanvon Technology, is the first consumer device to ship with Kaleido 4. The device combines the new e-paper display with advanced features such as optical character recognition (OCR), AI-powered voice and office apps, cloud library sync, and ripple-reduction technology. Its battery-free stylus offers 8,192 levels of pressure sensitivity, while a micro-etched tempered glass surface minimizes glare and parallax, mimicking the feel of writing on paper.

Hanvon positions the M10C II not just as a reading tool, but as a productivity device for document review, note-taking, and mobile meetings. The display’s oxide TFT backplane and optimized timing controller enable 27% faster refresh rates than the previous generation, improving page turns and scrolling.
