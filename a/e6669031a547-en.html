Customizable Word Clock with ESP8266 and NeoPixel LEDs

A 3D-printable grid for a word clock project compatible with ESP8266 and NeoPixel LEDs, featuring files for English, Italian and German.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Designer didldiprint has released a 3D-printable grid designed to support a word clock project using an ESP8266 microcontroller and WS2812B NeoPixel LEDs. The model, inspired by a tutorial from techniccontroller.com, displays time in full words in three languages: English, Italian, and German.

The design includes a TTP223 touch sensor as a substitute for a physical button, discreetly mounted above the last character of the first row. The rigid white support frame is taken from an IKEA RÖDALM model to prevent pressure on the glass or acrylic panel. A LightBurn file is provided for laser-cutting the front face from cardboard or plywood, with specific cutting parameters (0.28 mm layer height, 2 walls, 15 % infill, estimated print time of 1.8 hours for a single plate).

Files are available for download on MakerWorld at: https://makerworld.com/en/models/1000720-grid-for-word-clock-2-0-esp8266-with-ws2812b-neopi.
