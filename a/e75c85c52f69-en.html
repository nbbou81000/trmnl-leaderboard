Sound Reactive Paper Lanterns with LEDFx and ESP32

Turn paper lanterns into sound-reactive lighting with an ESP32 and LEDFx, all free and open source.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

This project turns ordinary paper lanterns into dynamic sound-reactive lighting. The setup relies on a Feather ESP32 board, a compact and powerful microcontroller, paired with the WLED firmware. WLED controls connected LED strips or lighting modules and provides a web interface for configuration.

To add sound reactivity, the open-source LEDFx software is used. It analyzes audio from a computer (via Wi-Fi) and generates synchronized light animations. LEDFx offers customizable settings to adjust visual effects based on volume or audio frequencies. The system can be powered via USB or a battery, making it easy to install anywhere.

The project is documented in a full guide, covering required components, assembly steps, and configuration settings. While no advanced programming skills are needed, familiarity with microcontrollers and Wi-Fi networks is helpful.
