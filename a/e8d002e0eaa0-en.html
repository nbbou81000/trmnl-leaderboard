EEW Maker Kit: A Fully DIY and Open-Source Spinning Wheel

An open-source kit lets you build your own electric spinning wheel, customizable and 3D-printable.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Engineer and inventor Maurice Ribble has launched a Kickstarter campaign for the EEW Maker Kit, an open-source kit designed to build a customizable, 3D-printed electric spinning wheel (eSpinner). Unlike commercial models, this version offers full personalization, from mechanical parts to embedded electronics.

The kit includes a custom TEC 3650 brushless motor, a steel spindle, a dedicated control board, and all necessary hardware. The 3D-printable files are released under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license, allowing modifications and community-driven improvements. Ribble emphasizes the project’s autonomy: "You’re not just getting a spinning wheel; you’re joining an active community sharing their own upgrades."

The eSpinner supports up to 8 oz bobbins, with a beta-tested 16 oz version in development. Its rotation speed ranges from 200 to 1,600 RPM, regulated by a custom control circuit that stabilizes speed based on the material. The system also features a tangle detection feature to automatically stop the flyer if the yarn snags. Users can adjust soft-start and soft-stop ramps via dedicated software.

The crowdfunding campaign offers the kit starting at $99, covering only the electronic and mechanical components. The 3D files are available for free on Printables. Shipments are expected to begin in June 2027 for "early access" backers, followed by August 2027 for others. Ribble, known for delivering on past promises, assures reliable production despite crowdfunding uncertainties.
