Hacker modifies a Renault car's time via the navigation system

A 2014 Renault Latitude owner bypasses the need for an SD card to adjust the dashboard clock.

Rasmus Moorats purchased a 2014 Renault Latitude, and the previous owner offered him a navigation SD card to update the dashboard clock. This solution seemed dubious, especially since online videos showed direct access menus for clock adjustments. Noticing the provided SD card contained outdated and unusable maps, he decided to forgo it entirely.

Unable to access the settings through standard menus, he set out to jailbreak the navigation system to manually adjust the time. His goal was to bypass the reliance on the SD card, often marketed as the sole solution by dealerships. After research, he documented his findings in a detailed post, explaining how he accessed hidden functions of the embedded system.
