ESP32-S3-Based Toddler Phone with E-Ink Display and Oversized Buttons

A simplified child-friendly phone with oversized buttons and an e-ink display enables calls to 4 contacts during predefined hours.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Designed by Allan Binder, this minimalist phone is aimed at young children to easily call their loved ones. Four oversized backlit buttons, arranged like arcade controls, automatically dial one of four saved contacts. Each button lights up only during the hours the recipient has marked as available, preventing unwanted calls.

The system includes customizable time slots per contact and time zone. For instance, a button dedicated to a grandparent only illuminates when they have confirmed they are ready to receive calls. This visual logic helps a child quickly learn that a lit button means they can call.

The project uses an ESP32-S3 board, chosen for its processing power and built-in Wi-Fi connectivity. The e-ink display shows information clearly and without flicker, making it suitable for young users. A demonstration video showcases the device’s operation and construction.
