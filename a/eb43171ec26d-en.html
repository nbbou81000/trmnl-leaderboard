Microchip Unveils MTCH3380P and MTCH3240P Capacitive Touch Controllers for Display-Free HMIs

Microchip introduces two new capacitive touch controllers designed for display-free HMI devices, supporting up to 64 touch keys.

Microchip Technology has launched the MTCH3380P and MTCH3240P, two new capacitive touch controllers designed for display-free human-machine interfaces (HMIs). Their key feature is built-in touch detection, which simplifies the creation of touch-sensitive buttons, sliders, or wheels. The MTCH3380P supports up to 38 keys in self-capacitance mode or 64 in mutual-capacitance mode, while the MTCH3240P handles up to 24 or 32 keys respectively.

These chips integrate 12 analog-to-digital converters (ADCs) for parallel scanning, enhancing responsiveness and noise immunity. They can detect touches through thick materials (up to 6 mm glass or 3 mm plastic) and work even when users wear gloves. Their robustness makes them suitable for home appliances, industrial control panels, EV charging stations, or outdoor kiosks. They also meet IEC/UL 60730 Class B functional safety requirements.

The controllers feature a patented charge-transfer acquisition engine and offer advanced functions such as adjacent key suppression (AKS), a PWM output for backlighting or haptic feedback (MTCH3380P only), and an I2C interface up to 1 MHz. Their operating temperature ranges from -40°C to +105°C, and their power supply varies between 1.8V and 3.3V. The MTCH3380P and MTCH3240P are available in 56-pin and 38-pin XQFN packages respectively.

Microchip provides evaluation kits (EV-MTCH3380P-DEV-PCB and EV-MTCH3240P-DEV-PCB) and configuration tools via mTouch Studio. The controllers are available starting at $0.84 (MTCH3240P) and $1.10 (MTCH3380P) for orders of 5,000 units, with minimum order quantities varying by packaging.
