PixelFonts: Fonts Optimized for Low-Resolution Screens

A condensed font family designed to maximize readability on tiny e-ink or LCD displays.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Small screens, such as e-ink displays or low-resolution LCDs, often struggle with readability due to limited space. PixelFonts addresses this by offering a family of condensed fonts tailored for low-resolution environments, ensuring each character fits efficiently without sacrificing clarity.

The fonts are compatible with the AdafruitGFX or U8g2 libraries, two widely used C/C++ frameworks for driving displays. Users can write text using standard library functions, while PixelFonts provides the optimized font files. The collection includes regular and 3D shadowed variants, along with multiple heights and weights. Accented characters are supported, though only in lowercase due to space constraints.

To encourage customization, each font is distributed as a BDF file, a standard format for bitmap fonts. This allows users to modify glyphs as needed, even without specialized tools. The author notes that pixel optimization isn’t limited to monochrome screens; techniques like subpixel rendering—commonly used on color LCDs—can further enhance the legibility of small fonts.
