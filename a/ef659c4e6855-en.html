Home Assistant Connect AUX-2: A Versatile Hub for Smart Home Integration

Nabu Casa unveils a multi-port hub to integrate non-smart devices into Home Assistant, featuring PoE, audio, and serial control.

Nabu Casa has launched the Home Assistant Connect AUX-2, a compact hub designed to modernize non-smart home devices. This multi-purpose box enables audio streaming, RS-232 serial device control, and Z-Wave adapter connectivity, while also serving as a Bluetooth hub. It supports Power over Ethernet (PoE), USB, or Wi-Fi power options.

The Connect AUX-2 targets two key use cases: extending the lifespan of existing equipment and enhancing the capabilities of newer devices. For instance, it can transform an analog audio system into a connected speaker synchronized with modern smart speakers, or integrate serial-controlled devices like climate systems and solar panels to optimize energy use. Users can also bypass software restrictions on certain TVs by controlling them via their serial port while powering them over PoE.

The hub features an upgraded digital-to-analog converter (DAC) compared to a previous version, offering both digital and analog audio outputs with automatic switching. It supports high-quality FLAC files and allows multi-device synchronization via the Sendspin protocol, compatible with Music Assistant. This approach avoids replacing functional devices while integrating them into modern smart home ecosystems.

For serial connectivity, the Connect AUX-2 supports RS-232 and RS-485 (via a $15 USB adapter), enabling connections to most industrial or domestic serial devices. A 3.5 mm connector simplifies integration with amplifiers or TVs. The device consumes less than 2 watts in standby mode, reducing the energy impact of added smart features.

Priced at $69 or €59, the Connect AUX-2 is available worldwide and aligns with a sustainability approach by extending the utility of existing devices.
