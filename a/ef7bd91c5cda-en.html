USB Mic Turned into Audio Instrument with Touchscreen and Effects

A USB mic paired with a 2.8-inch touchscreen and the Fruit Jam board enables real-time sound recording, visualization, and transformation.

A DIY project repurposes a USB microphone into an interactive audio instrument using the Fruit Jam board and a 2.8-inch touchscreen. Dubbed Touchdeck, the setup displays real-time audio levels and frequency spectrums while offering recording and vocal effect controls.

The USB mic streams 48 kHz, 16-bit audio. The interface runs on CircuitPython, with native C modules handling signal processing and rendering. The on-screen meters reflect digital signal levels in dBFS. Users can record up to five seconds of audio in RAM and apply effects like radio, robot, or echo to the original recording. The modified audio is output via DVI/HDMI.

A demo video showcases children making sounds before school and a baby, highlighting the tool’s creative potential. Additional learning guides cover USB host camera integration, sampled keyboard creation, and video music composition with the Fruit Jam.
