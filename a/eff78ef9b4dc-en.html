Weather Station on Arduino UNO R4: Optimizing Real-Time Apps

A weather station project migrates from GIGA R1 to UNO R4 with code and UI optimizations.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Developers have ported a weather station application originally designed for the Arduino® GIGA™ R1 WiFi to the more constrained Arduino® UNO™ R4 WiFi. The GIGA R1, featuring a dual-core STM32H747XI running at 480 MHz with 1 MB of RAM, ran the app under ZephyrOS—a real-time operating system (RTOS)—displaying weather data via a touch-based graphical interface.

The UNO R4 WiFi, with its single-core Renesas RA4M1 at 48 MHz and just 32 kB of RAM, presented two hurdles: shrinking the program to fit within 256 kB of flash and reducing RAM usage. Optimizations focused on simplifying the UI (reducing the color palette, minimizing image sizes) and switching display libraries. The result now runs “reasonably well” on the UNO R4 despite its limited resources.

This porting effort highlights how hardware constraints drive creative solutions, proving that an RTOS, graphical interface, and real-time data can coexist on modest hardware. A mindset that makes complex projects more accessible, even on low-end boards.
