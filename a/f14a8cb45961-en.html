DIY Finger Joint Cutter Using a Modified Jigsaw Blade

A maker turns a jigsaw into a precise finger joint cutter without laser or milling tools.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Instead of using a laser cutter to make finger joints, maker [bluesyann] demonstrates a DIY approach using a fixed jigsaw and a custom blade. The key is an oversized blade, created by epoxying together multiple standard jigsaw blades. This wide blade cuts a notch in a single pass, ensuring consistent dimensions across cuts.

A 3D-printed jig handles the spacing between each notch. Freshly cut pieces fit into the jig, aligning perfectly for the next cut. The setup relies on inexpensive, widely available materials: a standard jigsaw, off-the-shelf blades, scrap wood, and common hardware. The creator notes that some trial and error is needed to fine-tune the tool before achieving reliable results.

This method appeals for its simplicity and low cost, though commenters question the safety of modified blades and the inherent imprecision of jigsaw cuts. Alternatives like a table saw or router with a dedicated jig are suggested for faster or safer results.
