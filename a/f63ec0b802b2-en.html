Variscite Adds TPM 2.0 via Firmware to i.MX SOMs

Variscite offers a software-based TPM 2.0 module for its i.MX boards, activable via OTA update, to meet EU cybersecurity requirements.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Variscite has introduced a TPM 2.0 (Trusted Platform Module) security module for its NXP i.MX 6, 7, 8, and 9-based system-on-modules (SOMs). Unlike traditional hardware-based solutions, this feature is implemented in firmware, enabling immediate activation through an over-the-air (OTA) update.

This move addresses the requirements of the EU’s Cyber Resilience Act, which came into effect on December 11, 2026, mandating manufacturers to include mechanisms for protecting cryptographic keys and other sensitive data. The module, developed in partnership with SecEdge, leverages the security hardware already present in NXP chips, eliminating the need for hardware modifications to existing boards.

Variscite states that this solution allows customers to achieve compliance without redesigning their products. Sami Nassar, CEO of SecEdge, emphasizes that this approach combines proven security with practicality, crucial in the face of evolving cyber threats. The SEC-TPM module is now available for all i.MX 6–9 SOMs via Variscite’s Developer Center, and will be included by default in future releases.
