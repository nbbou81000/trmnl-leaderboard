Oldputer: An E-Ink Display for Retro Computers

A DIY project pairs a vintage e-ink display with an old computer via ESP32 to display data without eye strain.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Modern displays all look alike, with their thin panels and minimalist bezels, losing the distinctive character of old monitors. [Andrzej Górski] took on this challenge by creating Oldputer, a project that revives retro machines by integrating an e-ink display, a technology known for its readability and flicker-free experience.

The project’s core relies on an old e-ink display, deliberately chosen for its slow refresh rate, perfectly matching the pace of vintage computers. The device is driven by an ESP32 microcontroller, supplemented with a few LEDs for system status indicators. The entire setup is housed in an aesthetic case, turning the display into a functional yet decorative desk ornament. Users can display various information, such as time, system data, or custom messages.

To replicate this project, schematics and code are available on GitHub. Those interested in even older replicas can also explore the MCM/70 reproduction, another tribute to past computing hardware.
