A 3D-Printed Marble Clock That Scrolls Text

A bold project turns a marble clock into an infinite scrolling text display, entirely 3D-printed and motorized.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Creator Ivan Miranda took the concept of marble clocks a step further by designing a scrolling text display. Unlike traditional models limited to a fixed number of digits, this system uses a circular wheel where black and white marbles align to form characters.

The mechanism relies on two stepper motors: one rotates the main wheel, while the other operates an internal selector. This selector aligns the marble channels and chooses their color before they drop into dedicated slots on the wheel, reconstructing the desired text. The entire setup, fully 3D-printed and bolted together, forms an impressive yet functional structure.

Several technical challenges were overcome, such as adjusting infrared sensors disrupted by ambient light. Despite remaining limitations—like the need for manual wheel homing before use or gaps in the text due to the ratio of black and white marbles—the result remains legible and offers a potentially infinite display.
