Turn Your Robot Vacuum into an Intelligent Home Floor Plan Sensor

A LiDAR-equipped robot vacuum creates a precise indoor map, usable by Home Assistant to automate lighting, heating, or security systems.

Modern robot vacuums, such as the Switchbot K11 Plus, use LiDAR or optical sensors to map a home in real time. Using SLAM (Simultaneous Localization And Mapping) algorithms, they calculate exact wall boundaries, permanent furniture footprints, and room divisions, generating a metric floor plan with rare precision for homeowners.

This data, often locked in proprietary apps, can be extracted and integrated into Home Assistant via local integrations. The system converts raw coordinates into a dynamic vector or image representation, reflecting the home's physical layout in real time. Room segment coordinates enable targeted automations: zone-specific heating or cooling adjustments, lighting activation based on presence detection in a specific room, or security scenarios triggered by door, window, or motion sensors.

Advanced automations and interactive dashboards

Home Assistant leverages this data to create interactive dashboards, such as Lovelace floor plans that overlay sensor states (temperature, humidity) or allow targeted cleaning in a specific room. The map coordinates also serve as a reference for positioning other fixed smart devices, like Bluetooth beacons or mmWave radar sensors, within a consistent digital grid. The key benefit is local data processing, ensuring instant dashboard responsiveness and enhanced privacy by avoiding unnecessary transfers to third-party servers.

The robot vacuum thus becomes more than a cleaning appliance: it transforms into a mobile mapping sensor, powering complex automation logic and optimizing energy or security management in the home.
