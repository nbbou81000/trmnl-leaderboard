Arduino Nano XL Link: A Nano Boosted for Industry and Automation

Arduino unveils the Nano XL Link, an enhanced Nano with built-in Ethernet, CAN FD, and RS485 for industrial and automation use.

Read the article on the TRMNL Leaderboard →

Arduino has unveiled at electronica 2026 in Munich a significant upgrade to its Nano lineup: the Nano XL Link. This new board retains the compact form factor of the classic Nano but expands its capabilities to meet the demands of industrial environments, home automation, and building management.

The board is powered by a Microchip PIC32CK2051SG01144 microcontroller, featuring a 32-bit Arm Cortex-M33 core running up to 120 MHz, paired with 512 KB of static RAM and 2 MB of flash memory. While maintaining pin compatibility with existing Nano boards, the Nano XL Link adds a second row of headers on each side, greatly increasing its connectivity options. However, this modification prevents its use on a breadboard if the additional rows are populated.

Key to its industrial focus, the board includes built-in transceivers for three major industrial protocols: Fast Ethernet, CAN FD, and RS485. According to Arduino, these interfaces allow direct connection to industrial networks, motor drives, smart sensors, or touch panels without requiring external hardware. To ease integration into industrial settings, an accompanying Connector Carrier board is available. It adapts the Nano XL Link to a DIN 4U format, featuring screw terminals for CAN FD and RS485 buses, and an RJ45 port for Ethernet. The carrier board also serves as a reference design for custom hardware development.

The boards are on display at the event, but pricing and availability details have not yet been announced. Visitors can see them in action at the Qualcomm booth, Hall C5, Booth #439.
