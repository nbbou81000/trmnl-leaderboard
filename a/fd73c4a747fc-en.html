Organize Your Kitchen Utensils with 3D-Printed Gridfinity Storage

Two 3D-printed Gridfinity bins designed to store measuring cups and spoons, compatible with modular storage systems.

A user shares two 3D-printed Gridfinity bins designed to store measuring cups and spoons in the kitchen. Each bin features a dedicated slot, optimizing space and making utensils easily accessible. The larger container fits measuring cups up to 92 mm in diameter, while the smaller one accommodates measuring spoons up to 21.5 mm wide.

The 3D files are available online for printing. Users can customize the top-layer texture, called "Hilbert," directly in their slicer software. These storage solutions integrate seamlessly with existing Gridfinity systems, making them ideal for makers looking to extend modular organization into the kitchen.

The models are provided by enzo_dog and can be downloaded from MakerWorld. This project aligns with the #3DThursday initiative, which highlights 3D-printed creations and their practical applications, including electronics and workshop organization.
